- Source: https://github.com/mattpocock/skills (`skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md`)
- What was adapted: the interview style: map the topic as a tree, ask in rounds about the questions whose prerequisites are settled, give a recommended answer with each question, and don't act until understanding is shared.
