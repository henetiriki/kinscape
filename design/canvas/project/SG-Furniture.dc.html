Map furniture

The pieces that live on the map itself: the wordmark, search bar, map controls, event glyphs, the legend and the date dock.

States not drawn here follow board 04: hover tints, a 2px focus ring, disabled colours. A tap target under 44 keeps a 44 × 44 hit area.

Accessibility

Alt
Beside the name the icon is decoration: alt="". Alone (the phone pill) it is alt="KinScape".
Link
On the map the wordmark is not a link. If it becomes one, it goes to the map at the current date and is named "KinScape, map".

Accessibility

Names
"Zoom in", "Zoom out", "Fit South Africa", "Layers and filters". Icons are aria-hidden.
Disabled
At the zoom limit use aria-disabled="true" so the button stays in the Tab order and says why.
Focus
Each button rounds its own corners, so the card doesn't clip and the outside focus ring shows in full.
Keyboard
The map itself also takes + and −.

Accessibility

Group
The dock and the sheet are a region named "Map date".
Field
The date field is the only date readout: the slider's thumb is named for the date it sits on. Invalid dates: board 05.
Slider
Arrow keys step one unit at the chosen precision, Shift and an arrow ten. The thumb is a role="slider" with aria-valuetext set to the date.
Segmented
Each item is a button with aria-pressed; the group is labelled "Date precision" or "Playback speed in years per second".
Play
"Play through time", then "Pause through time" while it plays.
Grabber
A button named "Expand the sheet", with a 44 × 44 hit area.
