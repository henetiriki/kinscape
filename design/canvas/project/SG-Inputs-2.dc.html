Inputs and actions

Every button, field, chip, tab and checkbox row the sign-in and map screens use, at the sizes they use them, with their states.

States not drawn here follow board 04: hover tints, a 2px focus ring, disabled colours. A tap target under 44 keeps a 44 × 44 hit area.

Back link

Goes up one level: from People to the map, from a person to People. It is a link, so it can open in a new tab. Its states come from IconButton and the focus ring: no new colour.

Drawn by hand with the real back Icon. Hover and pressed are IconButton's; focus is the ring every control uses.

The row of buttons that saves or leaves a form page: the primary button, then the secondary one. It stays at the bottom of the window while the form scrolls under it. Drawn by hand: the design system has no ActionBar yet.

Button as a link

With an href a button is a link: the same look and states, no underline, and no disabled state, because a link can't be disabled. Use it when pressing goes to another page, like Cancel on a form. Drawn by hand: the design system's Button can't be a link yet.

Drawn by hand: the link form is not in the design system yet. It copies the real Button exactly. No disabled column: a link has no disabled state.
