Fan of Pins

The Pins of a Place spread around it. Each Pin is a 44 px target with its Event glyph. Its accessible name is the event type and the years of its span, such as “Residence, 1890 to 1905”.
Three Pins
Twelve Pins Ring 1 has a radius of 56 px and holds 7 Pins. Each next ring is 48 px larger. The Pins fill the inner ring first.
