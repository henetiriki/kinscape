Fuzzy dates

An exact year or month reads "In 1850" or "In March 1850". Lower case works too. Months are JAN to DEC. Years run from 1000 to 2999.

How it behaves

  1. The reading takes the helper's place and follows the typing. It's a polite live region, so it's read after a pause.
  2. Check on Enter or when focus leaves, never while the user types.
  3. A bad date keeps its text. The field gets a 2px error border, an error icon and aria-invalid. The message replaces the reading and is linked with aria-describedby.
  4. The message stays until the next check.
  5. Save checks every field. Nothing saves while one has an error, and an alert above the form counts them.
  6. A good date gets no success styling. The reading is the confirmation.
