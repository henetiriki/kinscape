Spacing and controls, three ratios

Each column starts with the 4px steps 4, 8, 12 and 16 (grey). Then it follows its ratio from the exact value, rounded to the 4px grid (green). Each name takes the column step nearest its value on main, and a tie takes the larger step. Each control takes its text from the 1.25 column of the Type scale board. Its height is its line height + 2 × inset + 2 × the hairline border. A control pads its sides with 16px. A dashed box marks a 44px hit area.
