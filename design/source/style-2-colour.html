Style guide · 02

Colour

Forest green carries the brand and every interactive thing. Burnt sienna is only for pins. Ratios are WCAG contrast, measured on white unless noted: 4.5 for text, 3 for borders, icons and focus rings. Each name is the token you type.

Brand

Primary actions, links, selection and focus.
--brand-pressed #163019 · 14.3:1 Pressed buttons and links
--brand-hover #1D3E20 · 11.9:1 Hover on buttons and links; a link underline thickens to 2px
--brand #264F29 · 9.4:1 Primary buttons, links (underlined 1px, offset 3px), selected point, app icon background
--brand-tint #E6EEE4 · fill only Selected tab, chip on, pressed secondary
--brand-tint-hover #D9E6D6 · fill only Hover on a chip that is on
--brand-tint-pressed #CCDDC8 · fill only Pressing a chip that is on

Accent

Only for pins and things that mean "a person was here".
--pin #9A3B12 · 7.0:1 Pins, clusters, event glyphs
map.hatch.colour #8B4A1C · map only Contested hatch, at map.hatch.opacity (34%). A MapLibre paint value, not a custom property

Neutrals

Text, borders and surfaces.
--ink #18211B · 16.5:1 Main text, dark banners
--ink-2 #2E3A33 · 11.9:1 Secondary text
--ink-3 #4A544C · 7.9:1 Helper text, overlines
--ink-disabled #6E776F · 4.6:1 Disabled text and icons
--icon-disabled #A3AAA1 · 2.4:1, disabled The glyph in a disabled icon button
--field-border #8E978C · 3.0:1 Field and checkbox borders (meets 3:1)
--line-strong #C9CFC5 · decorative Secondary button borders
--line #E2E5DD · decorative Dividers, disabled primary buttons
--track #D6DAD2 · decorative Date slider track, unselected year buttons
--segment #EEF0EA · fill only Segmented control track
--surface-2 #F4F5F0 · fill only Insets, hover fills, sign-in page
--surface #FFFFFF · fill only Panels, dock, popups, dialogs
--white #FFFFFF · 9.4:1 on brand Text and icons on --brand and --ink

Focus

One colour family, so anything green can be clicked.
--focus-ring #264F29 · 9.4:1 Same value as --brand. 2px outline, 2px offset, on every focusable thing
--focus-ring-on-dark #FFFFFF · 16.5:1 on ink Same value as --white. Focus ring inside dark banners

Over the map

Drawn on top of the map rather than on a panel.
--scrim rgb(24 33 27 / 0.45) · overlay Behind dialogs and phone sheets
--credits #24302B · 11.4:1 on land Credits line under the date dock

Third party

Google's own colours, for its sign-in button and nothing else.
--google-text #1F1F1F · 16.5:1 Google button label
--google-border #747775 · 4.5:1 Google button border
--google-mark #5F6368 · 6.0:1 The placeholder G mark in the mockups

System colours

Each comes as text, border-and-icon, and background. Always paired with an icon and words.
! Error That date doesn't exist.
--error #7E2316 · 8.6:1 on bg --error-border #B4412F · 5.6:1 --error-bg #FBEDEA --error-border-soft #E7B9B0 · sign-in alert border
! Warning No borders are mapped for 1750 yet.
--warning #6B4A00 · 7.3:1 on bg --warning-border #B7791F · 3.6:1 --warning-bg #FDF3DD
✓ Success Jurisdiction chain copied.
--success #1E5E32 · 6.9:1 on bg --success-border #2F7D46 · 5.1:1 --success-bg #EAF4EC
i Info Switched to month precision.
--info #1E4E6B · 7.8:1 on bg --info-border #3E7FA6 · 4.4:1 --info-bg #E8F1F7

The other map colours (sea, land, polity tints, rivers) are on the Map style board. They're MapLibre paint values under map in tokens.json, not custom properties.
