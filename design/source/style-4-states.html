Style guide · 04

Component states

How every control looks at rest, under the pointer, with keyboard focus, while pressed and when it can't be used. Toggles show off and on side by side.

Rules

  • Focus is a 2px brand outline, 2px outside the control, shown on keyboard focus only (:focus-visible). Fields show focus with a 2px brand border instead. In dark banners the ring is white.
  • Hover only darkens or tints. Nothing important lives only on hover: touch screens don't have it.
  • Pressed is the :active moment. Toggles keep their "on" look afterwards, through aria-pressed or checked.
  • Disabled controls keep their shape and drop to ink-disabled on line or surface-2. Say why nearby, or leave the control out.

Sizes

  • Touch targets at least 44 × 44px. A smaller visual (a 20px checkbox, a 36px chip) sits inside a 44px hit area.
  • Buttons: 50px on sign-in, 44px in panels and the dock, 40px inside cards.
  • Fields: 44px in the app, 48px on sign-in. Field text 15px, 16px on phones.
