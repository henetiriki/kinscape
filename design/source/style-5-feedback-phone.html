Feedback and validation

Errors block until fixed, warnings don't, success confirms something finished, info explains something the app did. Every one comes with an icon and words, never colour alone.

Behaviour

  1. Keep what was typed. Mark the field with a 2px error border and an icon, set aria-invalid , and link the message with aria-describedby .
  2. One line that says what's wrong and how to fix it, with the fix in the user's own format. No codes, no "invalid input".
  3. The map, slider and URL stay on the last good date, and the message says which date that is.
  4. Esc puts back the last good date. The slider, the step buttons and the Jump to buttons also clear the error.
  5. Out of range offers the nearest good date as a one-click fix.
  6. A good date gets no success styling. The map moving is the confirmation.
  7. Typing more detail than the current precision (1901-06 while on Year) switches precision and says so for 4 seconds.
  8. In the date sheet the message opens above the field, pointing at it, so it isn't cut off at the bottom of the screen.

Toasts and banners

Error toast. Stays until dismissed or retried.
Terrain didn't load.
Banner. Under the search bar, for app-wide problems that don't stop the map.

Where it goes: a field's problem goes next to the field; a problem with one part of the screen goes in an alert in that part; the result of something you just did goes in a toast; something wrong with the app goes in a banner. Errors and failures use role="alert" ; everything else uses role="status" .
