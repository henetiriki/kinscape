Inputs and actions

Every button, field, chip, tab and checkbox row the sign-in and map screens use, at the sizes they use them, with their states.

States not drawn here follow board 04: hover tints, a 2px focus ring, disabled colours. A tap target under 44 keeps a 44 × 44 hit area.

Part 1: buttons, text fields, chips and button links

Tabs

Switch between the Layers and Filters panels. The badge counts the filters that are on.

Measurements

  • Height · 44 on a phone, 40 in the desktop panel size.control · size.control-s
  • Width · Equal: each tab takes half the row (flex: 1) —
  • Bar · Desktop: padding 4, gap 4, 1px line under it · phone: 8 under the row, gap 8 to Close space.4 · space.8 · colour.line
  • Label · 14 / 20 · 600; selected brand-hover on brand-tint, others ink-3 type.label · colour.brand-tint · colour.brand-hover
  • Badge · 20 tall, min-width 20, fully round, brand, white 13.5 / 600; 8 after the label radius.full · colour.brand · type.chip · space.8
  • Radius · 8 radius.control
  • Close · 44 × 44, fully round, ink-3, 20px icon size.touch · radius.full · colour.ink-3

Accessibility

Role
A tablist holding two tab elements with aria-selected, each controlling a tabpanel. The mockups use aria-pressed buttons.
Name
The label plus the count: "Filters, 1 active". The badge alone would read as "Filters 1".
Keyboard
Tab moves into the tablist; Left and Right move between tabs and select them.
Close
aria-label="Close". It closes the sheet and returns focus to the Layers button.

Checkbox field

A checkbox with its name, and on layer rows a line of live detail. Sign-in uses the plain label form.

Measurements

  • Row · min-height 48; the whole row is the label, so all of it is the hit area size.field-l
  • Checkbox · 20 × 20 everywhere, radius 4, 1.5px field-border off, brand fill on size.checkbox · radius.small · colour.field-border · colour.brand
  • Gap · 12 between box and text space.12
  • Name · 14 / 20 · 600 ink type.label
  • Description · 13 / 20 ink-3; live facts, such as pins true on the date type.small · colour.ink-3
  • Label only · 15 / 22 ink-2 type.body · colour.ink-2

Accessibility

Role
A native input type="checkbox" inside its <label>, so clicking the text toggles it.
Name
The name. Tie the description with aria-describedby so it is read after the name.
Live
The description changes with the date ("8 of 214 true on this date"); it is not a live region, so it is read when focused, not on every change.
Keyboard
Tab to reach, Space toggles. Focus ring on the box.

Alerts

For a problem with one part of the screen. Use the untitled form when the one line says it all.

Measurements

  • Padding · 12 16 · radius 8 space.12 · space.16 · radius.control
  • Border, fill, text · 1px error-border, error-bg, error text colour.error-border · colour.error-bg · colour.error
  • Icon · 20 filled circle in error-border, white "!", 12 from the text space.12
  • Text · 13 / 20 type.small

Accessibility

Role
role="alert": a failed sign-in is an error. It is announced without moving focus.
Fields
Both fields get aria-invalid="true" and a 2px error-border. The password is cleared, with the helper "Password cleared after a failed attempt."
Icon
aria-hidden; the words carry the meaning.

Dividers

Separate groups that sit close together. Use space first; reach for a line when space alone doesn't read.

Measurements

  • Line · 1px line colour.line
  • "or" · 13 / 20 ink-3, 12 from each line type.small · space.12 · colour.ink-3
  • Footnote · 20 between rule and text; 13 / 20 ink-3 space.20 · type.small

Accessibility

Hairline
Decoration: a span or an hr with aria-hidden, so it isn't read.
"or"
The word is read; the two lines are aria-hidden.

Key hints

Desktop only: phones have no keyboard to hint at. See the desktop board.

Page header

The bar at the top of every page away from the map: the Wordmark icon, a link back to the page above, and the Avatar at the right. Drawn by hand: the design system has no PageHeader yet.

Measurements

  • Height · 64 · 60 on a phone layout.page-header · layout.page-header-phone
  • Padding · 0 24 · 0 16 on a phone size.gutter · size.gutter-phone
  • Gap · 16 between the parts · 8 on a phone space.16 · space.8
  • Wordmark · 32 · the icon alone on a phone Wordmark size 32
  • Divider · 1 × 28, line · none on a phone colour.line
  • Surface · white, 1px line below colour.surface · colour.line

Accessibility

Role
A header at the top of the page. The divider is aria-hidden.
Order
Wordmark, back link, Avatar: the reading and Tab order follow the bar from the left.
Avatar
The real Avatar. It opens the account menu, as on the map.

Back link

Goes up one level: from People to the map, from a person to People. It is a link, so it can open in a new tab. Its states come from IconButton and the focus ring: no new colour.

Measurements

  • Height · 44, the touch size size.touch
  • Padding · 0 12 0 8 space.12 · space.8
  • Icon · back, 16, 8 from the text, the colour of the text space.8
  • Label · 15 / 22 · 600 · ink type.control · colour.ink
  • Radius · 8 radius.control
  • Hover · surface-2 behind, as IconButton colour.surface-2
  • Pressed · brand-tint behind, brand-hover text and icon, as IconButton colour.brand-tint · colour.brand-hover
  • Focus · 2px outline, 2px outside focus.width · focus.offset · colour.focus-ring

Accessibility

Role
A real a with the href of the page above. An anchor, so the browser loads that page.
Name
The visible text, Map or People. The icon is aria-hidden.
Keyboard
Tab to reach, Enter to follow. Focus shows the ring only on :focus-visible.

Action bar

The row of buttons that saves or leaves a form page: the primary button, then the secondary one, side by side as on desktop. No phone screen uses it yet: the person form on a phone has no Save or Cancel today. Drawn by hand: the design system has no ActionBar yet.

Measurements

  • Height · 72 · a phone adds the safe area of the device below layout.action-bar
  • Row · up to 1088 wide, centred, 24 at each side · on a phone 12 above the buttons and 16 at each side, buttons at the top size.gutter · space.12 · size.gutter-phone
  • Buttons · M 44, primary then secondary, 8 apart size.control · space.8
  • Surface · white, 1px line above, above the page colour.surface · colour.line · z.action-bar

Accessibility

Order
Save, then Cancel, in reading and Tab order.
Save
type="submit" with a form attribute, because the bar sits outside the form.
Cancel
A link to the page above: the button as a link.
Space
The page keeps 72 free at its foot, so the last field can scroll above the bar.
