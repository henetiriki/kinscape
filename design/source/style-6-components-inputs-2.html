Inputs and actions

Every button, field, chip, tab and checkbox row the sign-in and map screens use, at the sizes they use them, with their states.

States not drawn here follow board 04: hover tints, a 2px focus ring, disabled colours. A tap target under 44 keeps a 44 × 44 hit area.

Page header

The bar at the top of every page away from the map: the Wordmark, a divider, a link back to the page above, and the Avatar at the right. Drawn by hand: the design system has no PageHeader yet.

Wordmark 32 · the icon alone on a phone Wordmark size 32
Divider 1 × 28, line · none on a phone colour.line
Surface white, 1px line below colour.surface · colour.line

Accessibility

Role
A header at the top of the page. The divider is aria-hidden.
Order
Wordmark, back link, Avatar: the reading and Tab order follow the bar from the left.
Avatar
The real Avatar. It opens the account menu, as on the map.

Back link

Goes up one level: from People to the map, from a person to People. It is a link, so it can open in a new tab. Its states come from IconButton and the focus ring: no new colour.

Drawn by hand with the real back Icon. Hover and pressed are IconButton's; focus is the ring every control uses.

Accessibility

Action bar

The row of buttons that saves or leaves a form page: the primary button, then the secondary one. It stays at the bottom of the window while the form scrolls under it. Drawn by hand: the design system has no ActionBar yet.

Measurements

Height 72 · a phone adds the safe area of the device below layout.action-bar
Row up to 1088 wide, centred, 24 at each side · on a phone 12 above the buttons and 16 at each side, buttons at the top size.gutter · space.12 · size.gutter-phone
Buttons M 44, primary then secondary, 8 apart size.control · space.8
Surface white, 1px line above, above the page colour.surface · colour.line · z.action-bar

Accessibility

Order
Save, then Cancel, in reading and Tab order.
Save
type="submit" with a form attribute, because the bar sits outside the form.
Cancel
A link to the page above: the button as a link.
Space
The page keeps 72 free at its foot, so the last field can scroll above the bar.

Button as a link

With an href a button is a link: the same look and states, no underline, and no disabled state, because a link can't be disabled. Use it when pressing goes to another page, like Cancel on a form. Drawn by hand: the design system's Button can't be a link yet.

Drawn by hand: the link form is not in the design system yet. It copies the real Button exactly. No disabled column: a link has no disabled state.

Measurements

Height M 44 · S 40 inside desktop cards (M 44 on a phone) size.control · size.control-s
Padding 0 16 space.16
Label M 15 / 22 · S 14 / 20, all 600, no underline type.control · type.button-s
States the Button's: hover, focus ring, pressed colour.brand-hover · colour.brand-pressed · colour.brand-tint
Disabled none: a link is never disabled —

Accessibility

Role
A real a with an href , not a button.

The visible label.
Keyboard
Tab to reach, Enter to follow. Space scrolls the page, as on any link.

Long text field

For a note of several lines, such as the notes on a Person. It has the look of a text field, resizes up and down only, and scrolls long content inside the box.

Measurements

Border 1px field-border; hover 1px ink-3; focus 2px brand, padding 1 less colour.field-border · colour.ink-3 · colour.brand
Radius 8 radius.control
Padding 10 12 space.12 · 10: no token
Text 15 / 22 type.body · type.body-l
Label 14 / 20 · 600, 8 above the field type.label · space.8
Resize Vertical only —

Accessibility

Label
A visible <label for> on a real textarea.
Helper
The text under the field. aria-describedby points at it.
Limit
maxLength stops the text at that many characters. The Person form uses 10,000.
Keyboard
Tab to reach. Enter adds a line. Focus shows the 2px brand border.

Fuzzy date field

For a date typed as GEDCOM text, such as a Person’s birth. A reading follows the typing. An error shows when the user leaves the field or presses Enter.

Measurements

Border 1px field-border; focus 2px brand; error 2px error-border colour.field-border · colour.brand · colour.error-border
Radius 8 radius.control
Height 44 size.field
Text JetBrains Mono 15 / 22 · 400, ligatures off type.mono-m · type.body-l
Label 14 / 20 · 600, 8 above the field type.label · space.8
Message 13 / 20, 8 under the field type.small · space.8

Accessibility

Label
A visible <label for> on a real input.
Message
One aria-live="polite" slot holds the helper, the reading or the error. The error is role="alert" and the input has aria-invalid and aria-describedby for the slot.
Keyboard
Tab to reach. Enter checks the text. Focus shows the 2px brand border.
