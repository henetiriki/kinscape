Surfaces and overlays

The cards, dialogs, sheets, menu, popup and callout that sit above the map, from the panel up to the modal dialog.

States not drawn here follow board 04: hover tints, a 2px focus ring, disabled colours. A tap target under 44 keeps a 44 × 44 hit area.

Part 1: surface, notice card, progress bar, dialog

Phone sheet header

The top of every phone bottom sheet: a handle, then one of three header rows.

Measurements

Handle 40 × 4, radius 2, line-strong, centred, 8 from the top edge 40 × 4: component size, no token
Padding 8 16 at the top; 8 (tabs) or 12 (title) from handle to header space.8 · space.16 · space.12
Title H2 24 / 32 · 700 type.h2
Close 44 × 44 round, ink-3, 20px icon; its right edge pulled 8 into the gutter so the icon lines up with the text column size.touch
Tabs row Tabs share the row, gap 8 to Close, 8 below, then a 1px line space.8 · colour.line

Accessibility

Heading
The title is a real h2 and labels the sheet's dialog.
Close
aria-label="Close"; Esc does the same.
Handle
Decoration on an open sheet (aria-hidden). On the collapsed date sheet it is a button, "Expand the sheet", which isn't covered here.

A short list of actions behind a trigger. Today only the account menu, opened from the avatar.

Measurements

Header "Signed in as" 12.5 / 18 ink-3, 4 above the email 14 / 600; padding 12 type.caption · type.label · space.4 · space.12
Item min 44; padding 4 12; icon 18, 12 to the label; radius 8. The two-line item is 52 tall size.touch · size.row-l · space.12 · radius.control
Label 14 / 20 · 600; second line 12.5 / 18 ink-3 type.label · type.caption
Hover surface-2 fill colour.surface-2
Divider 1px line, 8 in from the sides, 4 above and below colour.line · space.8 · space.4
Footer Last backup, 12.5 / 18 ink-3, padding 8 12 type.caption
Avatar 44 round, brand, white initial 16 / 600, raised shadow, no border size.touch · type.button · shadow.raised

Accessibility

Trigger
The avatar is a button named "Account menu", with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.
Role
role="menu" with menuitem children. The header and footer text sit outside the menuitems (role="none" or a labelled group) so arrow keys skip them.
Keyboard
Opening moves focus to the first item. Up and Down move, Enter activates, Esc closes and returns focus to the avatar. Tab leaves and closes.
Name
The menu is labelled "Account". Icons are aria-hidden.

Answers "what was this spot part of on the map date?" where you clicked: the Jurisdiction chain and the pins there.

Measurements

Header Overline "On this spot in {year}" · place name H3, Bricolage 22 / 28 · 700 · coordinates mono 12 / 16 ink-3 type.overline · type.h3 · type.mono
Close 36 × 36 round, ink-3, 16px icon, pulled 8 up and 8 right; 44 × 44 hit area size.control-xs
Chain rows Grid 76 / rest, gap 8; 8 above and below; 1px line between. Label overline; value 15 / 22 · 600; detail 12.5 / 18 ink-3 type.overline · type.title · type.caption · space.8
Pins here Inset: surface-2, radius 8, padding 12, gap 8; row min 32. Glyph 14 pin, 8 to the title 14 / 600, people 13 / 20 ink-3, date mono 12 ink-2 colour.surface-2 · type.label · type.small · type.mono · 32: no token
Copy chain Desktop: 36 secondary with the copy icon (board 06), 44 × 44 hit area. The phone look-up has no Close and no Copy chain size.control-xs

Accessibility

Role
Desktop: a non-modal role="dialog" named "Jurisdiction at this point". Phone: a region of the bottom sheet named by its overline.
Focus
Opening a popup with the keyboard moves focus to it; Esc or Close returns focus to the map.
Chain
An ordered list (<ol>), smallest unit first. Each row reads level then name.
Pins
Each pin is a link to its record. The glyph is aria-hidden; the event name says what it is.
Copy chain
Copies the chain as text and confirms with the "Jurisdiction chain copied." toast (board 05).

Callout

A message that belongs to one control and has to point at it: today, the error on the map date field in the dock.

Measurements

Box 340 wide · padding 12 · radius 8 340: component size, no token · space.12 · radius.control
Colours error-bg, 1px error-border, error text colour.error-bg · colour.error-border · colour.error
Text 13 / 20; first sentence 600 type.small
Fix link Phone: "Go to" the last good date, 4 below the text, as a Link at 13 / 20. It sits in the message, so it keeps its text height (DM.12.2 D3) type.link-s
Pointer 12 square turned 45°, error-bg with the box's border on its two outer sides, 7 below the box 12, 7: component size, no token
Shadow floating, as a popup shadow.floating
Place Opens above the field it belongs to, so it isn't cut off at the bottom of the screen —

Accessibility

Role
role="alert", with an id the field points at through aria-describedby.
Keyboard
Esc restores the last good date and closes the callout.
Pointer
Decoration (a styled span), never read.
