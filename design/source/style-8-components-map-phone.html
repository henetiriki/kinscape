Map furniture

The pieces that live on the map itself: the wordmark, search bar, map controls, event glyphs and the legend.

Wordmark

The icon, then "KinScape" in Bricolage Grotesque 700. Drop the name only where there is no room for it.

Measurements

  • Icon app/icon0.svg, never recoloured; corner radius 25% of its size (11, 9, 8) 44, 36, 32: no token · 11, 9: no token · radius.control (8)
  • Name "KinScape", capital S, Bricolage Grotesque 700 at 24 · 20 · 19; −0.01em at 24 and 19 type.h2 (24, without its tracking) · 20, 19: no token
  • Gap 12 · 10 · 14 space.12 · 10, 14: no token
  • Colour ink colour.ink

Accessibility

Alt
Beside the name the icon is decoration: alt="". Alone (the phone pill) it is alt="KinScape".
Link
On the map the wordmark is not a link. If it becomes one, it goes to the map at the current date and is named "KinScape, map".

Search bar

Top left on desktop, a full-width pill on a phone. Finds people and places; results are designed later.

Measurements

  • Desktop bar 52 tall, padding 0 8 0 10, gap 14, radius 12, raised; 24 from the left and 20 from the top of the map 52, 10, 14: no token · space.8 · radius.panel · shadow.raised
  • Divider 1 × 28, line colour.line · 28: no token
  • Field 340 × 44, surface-2, radius 8, no border, padding 0 10, gap 10; text 15; "/" key hint (board 06) size.field · colour.surface-2 · type.body · 340, 10: no token
  • Phone pill 52 tall, fully round (26), padding 0 4 0 12, gap 12, raised (0 6 20 / 0.14 on map-phone); 16 from the edges radius.full · space.4 · space.12 · size.gutter-phone · 52: no token
  • Phone field Bare, 44 tall, 16 text so the browser doesn't zoom size.field · type.body-l
  • Avatar 44 round, brand (board 07) size.touch

Accessibility

Role
Wrap it in role="search". The input is type="search", named "Search people and places".
Keyboard
"/" focuses the field from anywhere on the map (desktop). Esc clears, then leaves.
Focus
The mockups set outline: none and show no focus. Drawn here: a 2px brand border on the field (desktop) and a 2px brand ring inside the pill (phone).
Results
Search results are not designed yet (EXP-04).

Map controls

Zoom and fit on desktop; on a phone, one button opens the layers. Pinch and drag do the rest.

Measurements

  • Buttons 44 × 44 in the card · layers button 48 × 48 size.touch · 48: no token
  • Card white, radius 12, raised; 1px line between buttons radius.panel · shadow.raised · colour.line
  • Layers button white, radius 12, raised (0 6 20 / 0.14 on map-phone) radius.panel · shadow.raised
  • Icons zoom in and out 18 at 2px · fit 18 at 1.8 · layers 24 at 1.8, ink colour.ink
  • States As board 04's icon button: surface-2 hover, brand-tint pressed, icon-disabled at the zoom limit colour.surface-2 · colour.brand-tint · colour.icon-disabled
  • Place Desktop: 24 from the right, 88 from the top · phone: 16 from the right, 80 from the top size.gutter · size.gutter-phone

Accessibility

Names
"Zoom in", "Zoom out", "Fit South Africa", "Layers and filters". Icons are aria-hidden.
Disabled
At the zoom limit use aria-disabled="true" so the button stays in the Tab order and says why.
Focus
The card clips its children (overflow: hidden), which would cut the outside focus ring; drawn here unclipped. See the reply.
Keyboard
The map itself also takes + and −.

Event glyphs

One shape per event type, used wherever an event appears: chips, the legend, pin rows and the map.

Measurements

  • Colour pin, in every size colour.pin
  • Sizes 12 · 14 · 16; in running text they take the text size (13 legend, 13.5 chips, 14 pin rows) no token
  • Font Unicode characters set in Public Sans; the browser falls back for ✚ ✱ ▼ ▲ where Public Sans lacks them font.body
  • On the map SDF icons in pin with a 2px white halo (map style board) map.pin.colour · map.pin.halo-width

Accessibility

Hidden
Always aria-hidden="true"; the event name is always written next to it.
Shapes
Filled and open pairs (● ○, ■ □) differ in shape, not only colour, so they hold up in greyscale (NFR-06).

"Reading the map": how borders, contested claims, uncertain dates and event types are drawn. It lives at the foot of the Layers panel.

Measurements

  • Halo pin circle r5 at 0.6 with a 1.5 white stroke, in an r11 halo at 0.18 map.pin · 0.18: map.pin-halo.opacity is 0.24
  • Glyph row Birth, Residence, Marriage, Death only; a line above, 4 above the glyphs colour.line · space.4

Accessibility

Heading
The overline is a real h3 in the panel.
Swatches
Decoration (aria-hidden); the words say what each one means.
Colour
Each meaning differs in form too: solid, dashed, striped, haloed.
