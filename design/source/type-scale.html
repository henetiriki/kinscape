Type scale, three ratios

Each column grows from a 16px base. Step 400 is 16px, and step 400 + 100n is 16 × ratioⁿ. A line height is the size × 1.25 for a heading role and × 1.5 for another role, rounded to a multiple of 4px. A role with an arrow gets a new name. A role with no arrow keeps its name. The last number pair of each cell is the size and line height on main.
