# Plan: M1.2: Basemap and today's provinces

- **Spec:** #66 (Intent #56)
- **Requirement IDs:** MAP-01, MAP-02, MAP-05, MAP-06, BND-01, BND-06, NFR-08
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

All map data is Natural Earth 10m (public domain) at one pinned release. `npm run map:prepare` downloads it, clips it to southern Africa and simplifies all layers together with mapshaper, so shared edges stay identical. It writes land, coastline and rivers (`scalerank` ≤ 7) to `public/map/` and today's Boundary versions to `data/boundaries/today.geojson`. It also builds Public Sans SemiBold glyph PBFs with fontnik. The output is committed; CI downloads nothing.

A static route, `/data/polities`, serves the Boundary versions plus a polylabel point per name row, all with `validFrom` and `validTo`. One filter builder (reused in M1.3) applies the Map date to every polity layer. The "Polity borders" row switches fills, borders and labels together.

**Decisions for the tech lead:**

- **Province source:** Natural Earth admin-1, not the MDB. This avoids the MDB licence question, and MDB edges wouldn't meet Natural Earth's Lesotho and eSwatini.
- **Licence:** recorded from Natural Earth's `LICENSE.md` at the pinned tag. Please confirm the terms page agrees; I couldn't open it.
- **Start date:** the Spec's 2006-03-01 may be superseded by the Sixteenth Amendment (2009, Merafong to Gauteng). Which date? Lesotho and eSwatini start then too; M1.4 extends them back.
- **Dependencies:** `mapshaper` (MPL-2.0, dev), `fontnik` (native, dev), `polylabel` (ISC), pinned exactly.
- **Tolerance:** 500 m, coordinates to 4 decimals, recorded in a new optional Source field, `processing`.
- **Credits line (PO):** bottom right, 24px gutter, until the date dock exists. MapLibre's attribution control is off.

## Alternatives considered

- **MapLibre font-maker:** no dependency, but a manual web step nobody can repeat.
- **MapLibre's own polygon labels:** may repeat once per tile.

## Risks

- **Old lines:** Natural Earth may predate 2009 in places; noted in `processing`.
- **fontnik:** may compile from source on Node 24. Only `map:prepare` uses it.
- **Size (NFR-02):** under 800 KB of GeoJSON in total, reported in the PR.
