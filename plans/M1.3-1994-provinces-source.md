# Plan: M1.3: Source for the 1994 provinces

- **Spec:** #67 (Intent #56)
- **Requirement IDs:** BND-06, NFR-08
- **Status:** draft

## Approach

**Source:** Natural Earth 10m admin-1 states and provinces, release v4.1.0 (2018-05-21), South Africa's nine provinces. It is public domain: "All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data … are in the public domain" ([LICENSE.md at v4.1.0](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v4.1.0/LICENSE.md), checked 2026-10-20).

**Fit:** v4.1.0 holds the lines from before 2006. v5.0.0 replaced them ("Update South Africa admin-1 for recent constitutional changes", CHANGELOG, issue #347). Point checks in v4.1.0: Matatiele is in KwaZulu-Natal, the Umzimkhulu exclave in Eastern Cape, Fochville in North West. So it fits 1994-04-27 to 2006-02-28. Until 2006, the Interim Constitution's Schedule 1 set the provinces by magisterial district, and every amendment to it changed names only. The Twelfth Amendment (in force 2006-03-01, Proclamation R. 8 of 2006, Gazette 28568) moved areas between seven provinces. Today's lines (M1.2) differ from 2006–2009 only at Merafong, which moved to Gauteng on 2009-04-03.

**Join:** v4.1.0 and v5.1.2 share 1031 of 1032 Lesotho vertices and 340 of 341 eSwatini vertices. M1.6 clips the v4.1.0 provinces to South Africa's outline from v5.1.2, so the outer and Lesotho and eSwatini edges match today's exactly. It then simplifies them in M1.2's mapshaper pass: 500 m, coordinates to 4 decimals.

## Open decision

**2006-03-01 to 2009-04-02.** M1.6 (#89) draws the v4.1.0 shapes until 2009-04-02, with low confidence. That puts the areas moved in 2006 in the wrong province for three years.

- **(a)** Keep M1.6 as written.
- **(b)** Recommended: end the 1994 shapes on 2006-02-28 and draw today's shapes from 2006-03-01, with low confidence until 2009-04-03. Only Merafong is then misplaced. M1.6's summary and its 2009 switch check need editing.

## Alternatives considered

- **Municipal Demarcation Board:** its terms bar commercial use and passing on changed data. It has no 1994–2005 province set.
- **Stats SA census 1996/2001:** terms for reprocessed data unclear.
- **GADM:** "Redistribution or commercial use is not allowed without prior permission."
- **geoBoundaries, HDX:** current lines only, derived from MDB data.
- **OpenStreetMap:** no history before 2009, and ODbL is share-alike.
- **Wikimedia Commons 2006 change map:** CC BY-SA, not georeferenced.

## Risks

- **Coarse lines:** v4.1.0 puts Bushbuckridge and Acornhoek in Mpumalanga, though that area was in Northern Province until 2006. The Kokstad area also needs checking. M1.6 checks both against Schedule 1 and records any mismatch in `processing`. If a line is plainly wrong, it stops and asks.
- **District changes:** a change to a magisterial district between 1994 and 2006 could move a province line without any amendment to Schedule 1. This has not been checked.
