## Approach

**Source:** Natural Earth 10m admin-1 states and provinces, release v4.1.0 (2018-05-21), South Africa's nine provinces. It is public domain: "All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data … are in the public domain" ([LICENSE.md at v4.1.0](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v4.1.0/LICENSE.md), checked 2026-10-20).

**Fit:** v4.1.0 holds the lines from before 2006: Matatiele is in KwaZulu-Natal, the Umzimkhulu exclave in Eastern Cape, Fochville in North West. v5.0.0 replaced them (CHANGELOG, issue #347), so today's v5.1.2 lines are post-2009, as a hand check of five moved areas confirms. Until 2006, Schedule 1 set the provinces by magisterial district, and its amendments changed names only. The Twelfth Amendment (in force 2006-03-01, Proclamation R. 8 of 2006, Gazette 28568) moved areas between seven provinces. Matatiele's move, struck down in 2006 with the order suspended, was re-enacted from 2007-12-14 ([Thirteenth Amendment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteenth_Amendment_of_the_Constitution_of_South_Africa)). So today's lines differ from 2006–2009 only at Merafong, which moved to Gauteng on 2009-04-03.

**Join:** v4.1.0 and v5.1.2 share 1031 of 1032 Lesotho vertices and 340 of 341 eSwatini vertices. M1.6 clips the v4.1.0 provinces to South Africa's outline from v5.1.2, so the outer and Lesotho and eSwatini edges match today's exactly. It then simplifies them in M1.2's mapshaper pass: 500 m, coordinates to 4 decimals.

**Which shapes, when** (option (b), chosen by @henetiriki): v4.1.0 from 1994-04-27 to 2006-02-28, today's shapes from 2006-03-01, low confidence until 2009-04-03. Only Merafong is misplaced, for 2006–2009. M1.6's (#89) summary and shape-switch check need editing to match before it moves to `ready-for-dev`.

## Alternatives considered

- **v4.1.0 until 2009-04-02:** misplaces every area moved in 2006 for three years.
- **Today's shapes from 1994:** misplaces every area moved in 2006 for twelve years.
- **Municipal Demarcation Board:** terms bar commercial use; no 1994–2005 province set.
- **Stats SA census 1996/2001:** terms unclear.
- **IPUMS 1996 layer:** no terms stated.
- **GADM:** "Redistribution or commercial use is not allowed without prior permission."
- **geoBoundaries, HDX:** current lines only.
- **OpenStreetMap:** no pre-2009 history; ODbL is share-alike.
- **OpenHistoricalMap:** CC0, but no 1994–2009 provinces.
- **Wikimedia Commons maps:** not georeferenced.

## Risks

- **Coarse lines:** v4.1.0 puts Bushbuckridge in Mpumalanga, though it was in Northern Province until 2006. M1.6 checks Bushbuckridge, Groblersdal, Moshaweng and Kokstad against Schedule 1 and records any mismatch in `processing`. If a line is plainly wrong, it stops and asks.
- **District changes:** a magisterial district change in 1994–2006 could move a line without amending Schedule 1. Not checked.
