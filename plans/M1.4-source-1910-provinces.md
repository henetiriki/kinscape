# Plan: M1.4: Source for the 1910 provinces

- **Spec:** #68 (Intent #56)
- **Requirement IDs:** BND-06, NFR-08
- **Status:** draft

## Approach

**Source:** Natural Earth 10m admin-1 v5.1.2, already pinned by M1.2. Public domain per its [`LICENSE.md`](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/LICENSE.md), checked 2026-10-20. M1.8 dissolves today's provinces into the 1910 ones:

| 1910 province     | Today's provinces                                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cape of Good Hope | Western Cape, Northern Cape, Eastern Cape, west of North West |
| Natal             | KwaZulu-Natal                                                 |
| Orange Free State | Free State                                                    |
| Transvaal         | Gauteng, Limpopo, Mpumalanga, east of North West              |

Natural Earth has no line through North West. That line comes from OpenHistoricalMap ways 198546470 v4 and 199435025 v3 (decision 1). OHM data is CC0 "except where otherwise noted" in a `license` tag ([terms](https://www.openhistoricalmap.org/copyright), checked 2026-10-20). Neither way has tags.

Nothing is digitised by hand. M1.8 runs one dissolve and one cut, which fits its cap.

**Joins:** the shapes go through M1.2's mapshaper run and are simplified at 500 m together with the basemap, so their edges match Lesotho's and eSwatini's exactly. The OHM line's ends snap to Natural Earth's North West outline. Lesotho and eSwatini keep their shapes back to 1910-05-31.

**Confidence:** `low` for all four. Known departures from the real 1910–1994 borders:

- Walvis Bay belonged to the Cape until 1994-03-01, but Natural Earth draws it in Namibia, so it is left out ([Walvis Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walvis_Bay)).
- Kokstad and Umzimkulu (Griqualand East) were Cape in 1910 and are KwaZulu-Natal today, so Natal is drawn too large ([Griqualand East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griqualand_East)).
- Natural Earth's lines may predate 2009, as M1.2 recorded.

**Names and dates** (checked 2026-10-20):

- Cape of Good Hope, Natal, Transvaal and Orange Free State, "the same limits as the respective Colonies": South Africa Act 1909, s. 6 ([Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/South_Africa_Act,_1909/1960-06-03)).
- Start 1910-05-31: proclamation of 2 December 1909, London Gazette 28314 ([Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_London_Gazette/Number_28314)).
- End 1994-04-27: the Interim Constitution's commencement ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interim_Constitution_of_South_Africa)). The Union became a republic in 1961 and the provinces continued ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa_Act_1909)), so the shapes end in 1994.
- Basutoland becomes Lesotho on 1966-10-04: Lesotho Independence Act 1966, s. 1 ([legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1966/24/enacted)).

## Open decisions

1. **North West line:** the OHM ways don't name the map they were drawn from. OHM's API blocked the runner, so their changeset couldn't be read. Recommended: use them, and in M1.8's PR overlay them on Bartholomew's 1898 map ([Leventhal](https://geo.leventhalmap.org/maps/11074), "No known copyright restrictions"). Otherwise the tech lead digitises the line from that map by hand.
2. **Two sources:** the Spec asks for one. M1.8 would add Natural Earth's existing entry plus an OHM entry to `sources.json`.

## Alternatives considered

- **OHM's province relations (2691750, 2691753–2691755):** rejected because their licence is unclear. 49 ways come from HDX's COD-AB (Municipal Demarcation Board, CC BY-IGO on HDX) but are tagged `license=CCO 4.0`, which is not a real licence. 12 Orange and Vaal ways come from OHM's bulk import, which OHM credits to OpenStreetMap under CC BY-SA 2.0. Their outer edges also wouldn't meet Natural Earth's.
- **Digitising all four from a public-domain map:** hours of tracing that an agent can't do, edges that miss Natural Earth's, and over M1.8's cap.
- **MDB or HDX boundaries:** these are modern, and M1.2 avoided their licence.
