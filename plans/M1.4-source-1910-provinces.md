**A** is pinned to commit `fbdf94d8a561814510f5d62ec552d6d67f3488e6` (2022-04-23). Four features named as in the Act; 20 spot checks fit 1910. Its README: the author doesn't recall the sources; the Free State and northern Natal borders equal current provincial ones, Cape–Natal current municipal ones; "I think I digitized the Cape–Transvaal boundary from old Trig Survey maps."

**B** dissolves today's provinces, splitting North West between the Cape and the Transvaal along OHM ways 198546470 v4 and 199435025 v3.

**Joins:** the shapes are simplified at 500 m in M1.2's mapshaper run, with the basemap. For A, `prepare-map-data` first runs `-erase` with Natural Earth's Lesotho and eSwatini, so the edges match. A's Free State differs from Natural Earth's by about 0.7%, so lines shift at the 1994 switch unless M1.3 (#67) uses the same repo: decide both together.

**Confidence:** `low` for all four. Known departures:

- Griqualand East moved from the Cape to Natal in 1978 ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Griqualand-East)): A is wrong after, B before.
- Walvis Bay was Cape until 1994-03-01 ([Walvis Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walvis_Bay)): A keeps it eight weeks late; B never draws it.
- Lesotho and eSwatini keep today's shapes back to 1910-05-31. Not verified.

**Names and dates** (checked 2026-10-20):

- Cape of Good Hope, Natal, Transvaal and Orange Free State, "the same limits as the respective Colonies": South Africa Act 1909, s. 6 ([Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/South_Africa_Act,_1909/1960-06-03)).
- Start 1910-05-31: proclamation of 2 December 1909, London Gazette 28314 ([Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_London_Gazette/Number_28314)).
- End 1994-04-27: the Interim Constitution's commencement ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interim_Constitution_of_South_Africa)); they outlived the 1961 republic ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa_Act_1909)).
- Basutoland becomes Lesotho on 1966-10-04: Lesotho Independence Act 1966, s. 1 ([legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1966/24/enacted)).

## Open decisions

1. **Source (blocking).** CC0 covers A's author's rights, not the data traced: probably today's official boundaries, whose terms M1.2 left unconfirmed, much as OHM's relations below. Options:
   - **A, accepting the gap** (recommended), recorded in `sources.json` `processing`. B's line is unsourced too, and B fits worse.
   - **Ask A's author** first, in an issue the tech lead approves.
   - **B**, with the OHM line or one the tech lead digitises from Bartholomew's 1898 map ([Leventhal](https://geo.leventhalmap.org/maps/11074), "No known copyright restrictions").

## Alternatives considered

- **OHM's province relations (2691750, 2691753–2691755):** 49 ways come from HDX's COD-AB (Municipal Demarcation Board, CC BY-IGO), tagged `license=CCO 4.0`; 12 from OpenStreetMap (CC BY-SA 2.0).
- **Digitising all four:** hours of tracing, and over M1.8's cap.
