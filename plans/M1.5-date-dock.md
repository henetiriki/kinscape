# Plan: M1.5: Map date dock

- **Spec:** #88 (Intent #56)
- **Requirement IDs:** MAP-03, MAP-04, NFR-01
- **Status:** draft

## Approach

`MapShell` owns the Map date (a day number, starting today); `DateDock` owns the precision. All date maths lives in `lib/map/map-date.ts`: bounds, steps, slider snap and label, and parsing. The slider is a native range input valued in steps at the precision, so snapping is exact and the keyboard works. Each change reuses `polityFilters`: four `setFilter` calls, no fetch. Credits move under the dock; the first view gets `padding` for the panel and dock (DESIGN.md › Layout).

## Decisions

**D1 · Split the Spec** · Decides: Tech lead
About 800 changed lines with tests, over the 600 cap.

- **A.** This PR: dock, steps, precision, slider, date shown read-only, timing. A new Spec: typing, errors, fixes, Esc (rows marked F).
- **B.** One PR of about 800 lines.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because the Spec names this split.

**D2 · What is timed, and the CI threshold** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** From the Map date change (`performance.mark`) to MapLibre's next `idle`. E2E takes 20 steps across 2009-04-03, each under 250 ms; the PR reports a laptop run against 100 ms.
- **B.** Only the `setFilter` calls, under 16 ms; misses worker re-tiling.
- **C.** As A, at 100 ms.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because it times the whole redraw with headroom for software WebGL.

**D3 · How e2e sees which Polities are drawn** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** Builds with `NEXT_PUBLIC_E2E=1` (only `playwright.config.ts` sets it) put the map on `window`; e2e counts Polities with `queryRenderedFeatures`.
- **B.** Sample canvas pixels. No hook, but brittle.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because it is exact and production builds drop it.

**D4 · What the field shows at Year and Month** · Decides: PO
The mockups show `1901` at Year; the Spec's first check says "today's date".

- **A.** The date at the precision (`2026`, `2026-10`); the slider label gives the full date.
- **B.** Always `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because it matches the mockups and "Switched to month precision".

**D5 · The "No borders are mapped" warning** · Decides: PO
DESIGN.md lists it for the field; the Spec leaves the no-borders notice to M1.7.

- **A.** Leave it to M1.7.
- **B.** Build it now.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because M1.6 and M1.8 change which dates have borders.

- **Live drags:** `idle` waits for a pause, so only stepped runs are timed.
