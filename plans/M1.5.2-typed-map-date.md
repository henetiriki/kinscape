# Plan: M1.5.2: Typed Map date

- **Spec:** #103 (Intent #56)
- **Requirement IDs:** MAP-03
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

- **Keep the precision (Spec D6 A):** `DateField` commits a typed date at the finer of the typed and current precision, via a new `finerPrecision` in `lib/map/map-date.ts`. It must live in the field: the field sets its own text, and `1901` typed on Month at 1901-01-01 leaves the shown date unchanged, so `DateDock` could not correct it. Fixes keep their own precision, as now.
- **The note (Spec D7 A):** `DateDock` passes `notice` when the field's `onChange` brings a finer precision: "Switched to month precision." or "… day precision.". A `useEffect` clears it after 4 s; a new switch restarts the timer. `DateField` already draws it under the field as a status.
- **Tests:** `DateDock` and `MapShell` tests type through the dock; e2e types on the real map page.

## Decisions

**D1 · The note after a fix** · Decides: Tech lead

On a phone, "Go to today" at Year switches to Day. The design names the note for typing only.

- **A.** Show the note for any switch to a finer precision from the field, fixes included. One rule in `DateDock`.
- **B.** Typed dates only; `onChange` gains a flag saying which.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · Clearing the note early** · Decides: PO

The Spec says 4 seconds. Picking Year in that time leaves "Switched to month precision." up while Year is checked.

- **A.** Always 4 seconds, as the Spec says.
- **B.** Also clear it when the precision changes from the Precision control.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)
