# Plan: M1.5.3: Date dock on phone

- **Spec:** #104 (Intent #56)
- **Requirement IDs:** NFR-05, NFR-06, NFR-08, MAP-03, MAP-04
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

- **One form at a time:** `Dialog`'s `usePhone` moves to `foundation/usePhone.ts`. `MapShell` renders `Stage`; a phone gets the `layers` slot, the `Sheet` and `DateDock` with `sheet`.
- **Stage, phone:** the Layers button moves right, as the snapshots draw it. The `dock` slot loses its fixed height and stacks the credits above the sheet, collapsed or expanded (Spec D5).
- **Year on a phone (Spec D7):** with `sheet`, `DateDock` uses Year and shows no note. `DateField` gains `keepPrecision`: a typed date commits at the field's precision, so 1901-06 reads 1901 even from 1901-01-01.
- **Map padding:** `MapView` takes `padding` from `freeArea(phone)` in `Stage.tsx`, built from the overlay tokens. Creation reads `matchMedia`, since the server renders desktop; later changes call `setPadding`.

## Decisions

**D1 · Desktop placement** · Decides: Tech lead

`Stage`'s tokens move the dock 24px left and 4px up, and the panel 64px down, under where search will go.

- **A.** Desktop uses `Stage` too.
- **B.** Keep today's desktop positions; `Stage` on phones only.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · The phone Layers surface** · Decides: Tech lead

The snapshot draws a modal sheet from 76px down, over a scrim.

- **A.** `Sheet` titled "Layers" holding the Polity borders row, with scrim, Esc and focus return; only as tall as its row.
- **B.** `LayersPanel` in `Stage`'s phone `panel` slot (76px to the bottom), non-modal, with a Close button.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D3 · "Go to …" fixes on a phone** · Decides: PO

Spec D7 covers typing. A fix such as "Go to 1901-02-28" carries Day precision, which a phone can't undo.

- **A.** Fixes stay on Year too: the date moves, the field reads 1901.
- **B.** Fixes switch precision as on desktop.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D4 · MapLibre's zoom buttons and scale on a phone** · Decides: PO

Its zoom buttons (top right) overlap the Layers button; the scale sits behind the sheet.

- **A.** Hide both on a phone; pinch zooms.
- **B.** Move both between the Layers button and the sheet.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)
