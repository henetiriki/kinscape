# Plan: M1.5.4: Dock date error callout position

- **Spec:** #281 (Intent #56)
- **Requirement IDs:** MAP-03
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

Spec D1 B pins the callout to the top of the dock. Both mockups ([map-date-invalid](../design/map-date-invalid.jpg), [phone](../design/map-date-invalid-phone.jpg)) draw it 12px above the dock's top edge, over the map, with its pointer on the field. Today it sits 12px above the field, so it overlaps the dock's top 16px of padding.

- **Lift:** `DateField` already offsets the callout and pointer by `--callout-lift`. `DateDock` sets it to the dock's top padding plus 12px (28px), and to the sheet's 36px above the row plus 12px (48px) on a phone.
- **Width (D1):** `DateField`'s `.callout` takes `width: var(--callout-width, auto)`; the dock sets it.
- **Phone:** Play moves into the field's `after`, as `DateCase` draws it, so the callout spans the sheet's full width.
- **Design:** `design/DESIGN.md` › Map date field, rule 3, says where the callout sits. The kit's own text stays, since `style-5-feedback` is checked against its snapshot.

## Decisions

**D1 · How wide the dock's callout is** · Decides: PO

Spec D1 B says "spanning the dock", but the desktop mockup draws it 340px wide, from the Back button. The dock is about 1,050px wide.

- **A.** 340px from the Back button, as the mockup draws it.
- **B.** The dock's full width, edge to edge. The mockup then needs redrawing in Claude Design.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · The phone callout and the credits line** · Decides: PO

On a phone the credits line sits just above the sheet. A callout 12px above the sheet covers it while the error shows. The phone mockup draws no credits line.

- **A.** Cover the credits until the error clears.
- **B.** Lift the callout above the credits line too.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Alternatives considered

- **Position from the dock:** make the dock the callout's containing block. The pointer lives in the field's anchor, so it would need a second offset anyway.
