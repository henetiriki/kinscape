## Approach

`map:prepare` also downloads Natural Earth v4.1.0 admin-1, clips its nine provinces to South Africa's v5.1.2 outline and simplifies everything in one mapshaper run, as [M1.3](M1.3-1994-provinces-source.md) _Join_ says. It writes `data/boundaries/1994.geojson`; `today.geojson` gains a low-confidence version per province (D1). Lesotho and eSwatini start on 1994-04-27. `polities.json` gets the rename rows from M1.3 _Renames_ plus Swaziland (D3). `polityCollection` emits one label per name row and overlapping Boundary version, dated to their overlap, so the label moves when the shape changes.

## Risks

- **Glyphs:** ranges are 0–511; an en dash would need 8192–8447.
- **Long label:** Pretoria-Witwatersrand-Vereeniging wraps at the label's max width.
- **Size:** about 500 KB of GeoJSON, under NFR-02's 800 KB.
- **v4.1.0 keys** may differ from v5.1.2's; the script's key check fails loudly.
