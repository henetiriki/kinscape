# Plan: M1.7: Pre-1910 notice

- **Spec:** #90 (Intent #56)
- **Requirement IDs:** MAP-03, NFR-01
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

`boundaryCoverage(data)` merges every Boundary version's dates into sorted spans. `page.tsx` passes them to `MapShell`, so the first date and any gap come from `data/`, not a constant, and the browser gets a few numbers, not GeoJSON. `MapShell` puts a `NoticeCard` in the `Stage` overlay slot when "Polity borders" is on and no span holds the Map date; its button sets the Map date to the first span's start. `DateDock` takes `hasBorders(day)` and, after a typed commit to a day without borders, shows the warning as the field's `notice`. The warning belongs to the day it was set for, so Jump, a step or the slider drops it. With no spans at all, no card shows.

## Decisions

**D1 · Card at the top of the overlay slot** · Decides: Tech lead

The overlay slot centres its content; the card sits at the slot's top edge (88 desktop, 144 phone), as in `map-empty-date`.

- **A.** Add `overlayAt='top'` to `Stage`; centred stays the default for the designed loading and empty-tree cards.
- **B.** Top-align the slot always; the centred cards add an option when they're built.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · How long the date-field warning lasts** · Decides: Tech lead

In both options the warning replaces "Switched to … precision." when a typed date needs both.

- **A.** 4 seconds, like the precision note, on `DateDock`'s timer; any Map date change clears it sooner.
- **B.** Until the Map date next changes.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D3 · The card's icon** · Decides: Tech lead

The Spec names no icon.

- **A.** `layers`, the Layers button's icon: the card is about a layer.
- **B.** `info`.
- **C.** `place`, as on the pins card.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Risks

- **Existing e2e:** "typing 1901-06 switches to Month" now gets the warning; it moves to 2001-06.
- **Two `status` regions** while a typed date shows the card and the warning; tests select by text.
- **Union provinces** don't exist until M1.8, so the e2e asserts that some Polities draw after Jump, not a count.
