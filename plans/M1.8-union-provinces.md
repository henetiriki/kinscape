# Plan: M1.8: 1910 Union provinces

- **Spec:** #91 (Intent #56)
- **Requirement IDs:** MAP-06, BND-01, BND-02, BND-06, BND-07, NFR-01, NFR-08
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

`map:prepare` also downloads `provinces-1910.geojson` from afrith/historical-boundary-data at commit `fbdf94d` ([M1.4](M1.4-source-1910-provinces.md)). The same mapshaper run erases Natural Earth's Lesotho and eSwatini from it, clips it (D2) and simplifies it with everything else, so shared edges stay identical. The script writes `data/boundaries/1910.geojson`: one low-confidence version per Union province, 1910-05-31 to 1994-04-27, and fails if the file's four names differ from the Act's. Lesotho and eSwatini start on 1910-05-31 (D3). `polities.json` gets four new Polities (D1), Basutoland to 1966-10-04 and Swaziland from 1910-05-31. `features.ts` needs no change: labels already follow name and version overlaps.

## Decisions

**D1 · Polity ids** · Decides: Tech lead

Ids are hard to change once Pins (M2) cite them. The 1994 `free-state` is already named Orange Free State in 1994–95.

- **A.** `cape-of-good-hope`, `natal`, `orange-free-state`, `transvaal`. M4 can extend them back to the colonies if BND-03 says they continue.
- **B.** `union-cape`, `union-natal`, `union-orange-free-state`, `union-transvaal`: plainly 1910 to 1994, but wrong if M4 extends them.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · Coastline of the Union shapes** · Decides: Tech lead

afrith's coast isn't Natural Earth's, so fills could spill into the sea or stop short of the basemap's coast. M1.4 planned no clip.

- **A.** Clip to Natural Earth land in the same run. Walvis Bay stays, as it is land.
- **B.** No clip, as M1.4 says; slivers along the coast.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D3 · Lesotho and eSwatini before 1994** · Decides: Tech lead

The Spec's D1 accepts their shapes back to 1910 as unverified.

- **A.** Add a second version each, 1910-05-31 to 1994-04-27, low confidence; high from 1994 as now.
- **B.** Move the current version's start to 1910-05-31, high throughout.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Risks

- **Line shift at 1994:** the Free State differs by about 0.7%; accepted in the Spec.
- **Size:** about 100 KB more GeoJSON, under NFR-02's 800 KB.
- **Download:** 10.8 MB at prepare time only; a pinned SHA can't change, and HTTP errors fail the run.
