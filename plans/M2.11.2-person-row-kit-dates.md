## Approach

A helper, `app/components/actions/dates.ts`, builds the line, its hidden words and the spouse date text. `PersonRow` and the new `RelationshipRow` and `PickerRow` use it. `app/components` cannot import `lib/domain`, so `app/people/page.tsx` sends each date's short text and reading (D16). With `relationshipsHref`, `PersonRow` is a `div` with two links.

## Deviations

- An extra part, `DateText`, shows one line and its hidden words for the three rows. It takes `dates` or `spouse` and builds both texts. `bundle.test.ts` lists it as an inner part.
- `PersonDates` and `SpouseDates` are in `types.ts`. The new kit sections are in `rows.tsx`.
- The part 1 link on the desktop kit stops at "Person row", so the part 1 snapshot stays valid.
- D17: the screen shows "Mar. 1875, div. 1890", "Mar. 1875" or "Div. 1890". A screen reader hears "Married in 1875, divorced in 1890".
- `Section` takes an optional `part`, so the new sections show on no phone part (D19).
- `PickerRow` is a `button`, so a route can pick a Person.
- `RelationshipRow` has an "Edit" link, not a button. On `Person-relationships`, "Edit" no longer opens the dialog on the board.
- `Person-relationships` keeps "c. 1856–1930" in the text under its title.
- D20: `?part=3` is desktop part 3 and phone part 3. `SG-Inputs` and `SG-Inputs-2` say "of 3", and `SG-Inputs-2` links to part 3.
