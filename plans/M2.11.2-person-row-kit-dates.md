# Plan: M2.11.2: Person row kit and Dates column

- **Spec:** #536
- **Requirement IDs:** [PPL-01](../docs/requirements.md#ppl-01), [PPL-02](../docs/requirements.md#ppl-02), [NFR-05](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-05)

## Approach

A helper, `app/components/actions/dates.ts`, builds the line, its hidden words and the spouse date text. `PersonRow` and the new `RelationshipRow` and `PickerRow` in `actions` use it. `app/components` cannot import `lib/domain`, so `app/people/page.tsx` sends each date's short text and reading (D16). With `relationshipsHref`, `PersonRow` is a `div` with two links.

## Decisions

- **A.** Two strings for each date: the short text and the reading (`born`, `bornReading`, `married`, …). Empty is unknown. `PersonSummary` takes the same fields.
- **B.** The server builds the line and its words.
- **Other:** your own.

## Deviations

- An extra part, `DateText`, shows one line and its hidden words for the three rows. `bundle.test.ts` lists it as an inner part.
- The types `PersonDates` and `SpouseDates` are in `app/components/actions/types.ts`.
- The new kit sections are in `app/design-kit/inputs/rows.tsx`.
- The part 1 link on the desktop kit stops at "Person row", so the part 1 snapshot stays valid.
- D17: a screen reader hears "Mar. about 1875, div. in 1890" (the PO's "Mar." and "div." with the full readings).
- `Section` takes an optional `part`, so the new sections show on no phone part (D19).
- `PickerRow` is a `button`, so a route can pick a Person.
- `e2e/inputs.spec.ts` allows 9,500px for part 2. `design/screens.json` heights wait for step 9.
