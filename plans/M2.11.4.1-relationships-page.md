## Approach

The route `app/people/[id]/relationships/page.tsx` reads in two steps through `treeAction`. First it reads the Person, so a failed link read keeps the name (D6). Then it lists the Relationships, keeps the Person's, and reads each other Person by id. A pure function groups the links and sorts each group with `byName`. A client view, `PersonRelationships`, draws the board inside `EditOnComputer`. Three `/screens` routes give the boards synthetic data.

## Deviations

- `groups.mts` also exports `relatedPersonIds`, so the page reads only the related Persons. `RelatedPerson` is in `relationships/types.ts`.
- The page catches a failed read of the session, as the People page does. The notice then has no name, no email and the initial "?".
- `dates.mts` also holds `spouseDates`.
- `PersonRelationships` takes a union of props: `failed` has no `groups`.
- `features/people/index.ts` exports the two row types.
- The `private, no-store` check is a separate e2e test, not in the header loop.
- The session edited the `person-relationships` board, because the tech lead asked for it (D7). The Render workflow renders its snapshots and JPGs.
- The tech lead also asked for a name in the subtitle in the `title` style, and for the "Edit" link of `RelationshipRow` to look like the one of the People row (with its arrow) and to end in line with "Add".
- `RelationshipRow` is in `CONTROLS`, so the design system steps 2 to 5 ran. The tech lead approved this departure from the Spec's "no design system step". The design system is at version 81 and the canvas copy follows. Step 9 renders `style-6-components-inputs-3` as well.
