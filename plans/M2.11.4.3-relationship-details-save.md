# Plan: M2.11.4.3: Relationship dialog: details and save

- **Spec:** #846
- **Requirement IDs:** [PPL-02](../docs/requirements.md#ppl-02), [NFR-05](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-05)
- **Status:** draft

## Approach

A pick in `RelationshipPicker` calls a new `onPick`. `PersonRelationships` then shows a new client part, `RelationshipDetails`, in the same `Dialog`. Step 2 makes a UUIDv7 id at each pick, so "Try again" repeats the same create. Its form holds a `Segmented` "Type of parent" (or "Type of child"), or the two `FuzzyDateField`s "Marriage" and "Divorce". The other Person is the parent, child or `personBId`; this Person is the other end.

"Save" calls the action. An ok result closes the dialog. The action's `revalidatePath` then redraws the groups. A refusal, `divorce-before-marriage`, `conflict`, `not-found` or `tree-full` shows an `Alert` at the top of the body. A thrown call or `invalid` shows the error `Toast` with "Try again". A new `relationship-messages.ts` maps each code to the board's title and line. The `cycle` line names the other Person. `relationship-messages` gets no screen: it is a table of texts, and a unit test checks each text.

The page sends each entry with its `Relationship` and `version`, so "Edit" fills the form and the update names the version that the page read (D1).

## Decisions

**D1 · How "Edit" opens the dialog** · Decides: Tech lead

`RelationshipRow` gives "Edit" as an `ArrowLink` with an `href` only.

- **A.** The `href` is `/people/{id}/relationships?edit={relationshipId}`. The page parses the id with `parseRelationshipId` and opens the dialog if a group holds it. Close and "Cancel" replace the address without `edit`. No shared part changes.
- **B.** `RelationshipRow` gets `onEdit` and draws a button. This changes a design system input. Steps 2 to 7 of Changing the design system run, so the Spec text "no design system step runs" changes.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because the Spec keeps its scope and a reload keeps the dialog open.

**D2 · Texts that no board draws** · Decides: PO

The boards draw "Edit marriage" and "Change the dates." only.

- **A.** The edit of a parent is titled "Edit parent", and of a child "Edit child". Both have the line "Change the type." An empty "Marriage" shows the Birth helper of the Person form.
- **B.** A design follow-up draws them first. It blocks this Spec.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because each text follows a drawn one.

**D3 · The actions of `conflict` and `not-found`** · Decides: Tech lead

The `relationship-messages` board gives "Reload" and "Go to People" in its Action column. `Alert` has no action.

- **A.** The button shows under the alert, as on the Person form's conflict. "Reload" reads the page again. "Go to People" links to `/people`.
- **B.** The alert shows no action.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because it follows the board and the `person-conflict` screen.

## Risks

- **A wrong board.** The `relationship-edit-spouse` row reads "Mar. 1875" for van Wyk, but its dialog reads "1875" and "In 1875". The app shows `MAR 1875` and "In March 1875". The build raises a Bug that names the board and the difference, and hides nothing in code.
- **The toast and the modal.** The `<dialog>` makes the page inert. The toast goes inside the dialog, at the board's place at the foot of the screen.
- **Size.** About 480 lines without tests, as M2.11.4.2 was.
