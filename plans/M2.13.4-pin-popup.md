# Plan: M2.13.4: Pin popup on the map

- **Spec:** #862
- **Requirement IDs:** [PIN-02](../docs/requirements.md#pin-02), [NFR-05](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-05)
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

A shared `PinPopup` in `app/components/overlays/` builds the two draft boards: a floating form and a sheet form, each loaded, loading and failed. `MapView` reports a click on a Pin, a click on empty map and the pixel point of the open Pin on each map move. `pinsLayer` adds the selection ring as two circle layers that its filter limits to the open Pin. `MapShell` places the popup at the point on a desktop, and in the date sheet on a phone. `MapWithPins` holds the open Pin, and mounts the details query only when a Pin opens (#861). A `/screens` page builds the four boards with the components that the boards mount.

## Decisions

**D1 · The size of the Spec** · Decides: Tech lead · **Blocking**

The Spec estimates 650 lines. M2.13.2 (#1102) had the same estimate and changed about 1,780 lines. This plan counts about 1,400: the component and the design system about 500, the map wiring and e2e about 650, the screen page and entries about 250.

- **A.** Build it in one PR. Cost: more than twice the cap of about 600 lines.
- **B.** Split in two: (1) `PinPopup`, the `DateDock` slot, the token, the Components text and the design system steps; (2) the map wiring, the ring, the screen page, `screens.json` and the deletion of the draft boards. Cost: one new Spec, and #862 keeps part 2.
- **C.** Split in three: part 2 of B without the screen page and `screens.json`. Cost: two new Specs.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · The gap between a Pin and its popup** · Decides: Tech lead

The boards put the popup 30 px left of its Pin. `layout.popup-gap` is 14 px. The click popup of #868 takes the frame of `draft-pin-popup` (#860 plan D1) at a bare point, with no value for its gap.

- **A.** A new token `layout.pin-popup-gap`, 30 px. Cost: one token and its note.
- **B.** A literal 30 px in `PinPopup.module.css`, as the part's own dimension. Cost: #868 cannot share it.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D3 · "Edit" on the boards** · Decides: Tech lead

`map-pin-popup` and `map-pin-popup-loading` show "Edit". The Spec builds no "Edit" (#884 builds it). The check then sees about 2,600 pixels that differ.

- **A.** The popup shows an inert "Edit", as "Add a pin here" is inert until #869. Cost: the build rewords the "Add a pin here" line of #862; #884 does not change.
- **B.** `PinPopup` shows "Edit" only with an `onEdit`. The map passes none. `screens.json` ignores the area of the button until #884. Cost: the check skips the area.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D4 · The Pins and the ring on the boards** · Decides: Tech lead

The check hides the map (`svg[role="img"]`). The base boards draw the Pins and the ring in a second svg with `aria-hidden`, so the check sees them. The app draws them on the canvas, which the check hides. On `map-pin-popup-phone` they are about 0.6% of the screen. The limit is 0.5%.

- **A.** In step 7, the local session moves the Pins and the ring into the `role="img"` svg of `Map-pin-count.dc.html` and `Map-pin-fan-phone.dc.html`. The look does not change. Cost: two base boards change.
- **B.** `screens.json` ignores the area of each Pin and the ring. Cost: four `ignore` lists that the check skips.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D5 · Where the phone sheet goes** · Decides: Tech lead

`map-pin-popup-phone` draws the Map date and the Pin details in one sheet. `draft-pin-popup-phone` draws the details alone.

- **A.** `DateDock` gets a `details` slot in its sheet form. With details, the sheet opens and shows them under the slider. Cost: a change to `DateDock`, which is in `CONTROLS`.
- **B.** The details open in their own `BottomSheet` over the dock. Cost: the app differs from `map-pin-popup-phone`, and the tech lead raises a Bug for the board.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D6 · The ring on the count marker** · Decides: Tech lead

#862 D3 says that a count marker shows the selection ring once #863 builds it. #863 › Behaviour does not name the ring.

- **A.** The build adds a line to #863 › Behaviour: the count marker of the open Pin's Place shows the selection ring (#862 D3). Cost: one reword.
- **B.** Leave #863. Cost: its plan must find #862 D3.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D7 · The panel on the screen page** · Decides: Tech lead

The boards mount `Panel` with Layers and Filters tabs, `LayerGroup` rows and `Legend`. The live map mounts `LayersPanel` until #1057.

- **A.** The screen page mounts the panel of the boards. Cost: it differs from the live map until #1057.
- **B.** Keep the four boards in `NO_ENTRY` until #1057. Cost: an acceptance check of #862 changes.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Order of work

1. Record the answers. Reword the issues that they name ([reword step](../.github/agent/build.md#reword)).
2. Build `PinPopup` test first. Then the `DateDock` slot and the token.
3. Add `PinPopup` to `CONTROLS` with its card states. Write its Components text from each note of the two draft boards.
4. Run `npm run design-system` (step 2 of [Changing the design system](../design/CANVAS.md#changing-the-design-system)). Push.
5. The tech lead, or a local Claude Code session that the tech lead starts, runs steps 3 to 5 and step 7. Step 7 edits `Map-pin-count.dc.html` and `Map-pin-fan-phone.dc.html` (D4), with the [board swap prompt](../design/COMPONENTS.md#board-swap-prompt).
6. Build the ring, the click and the follow in `pinsLayer` and `MapView`. Then `MapShell` and `MapWithPins`.
7. Build the screen page and the `screens.json` entries.
8. Check each note of both draft boards against the Components text, the card states and the code. If each note is there, delete both boards ([deletion rule](../design/CANVAS.md#who-deletes-a-generated-file)).
9. The tech lead runs Design: Render snapshots. Run all checks. Compare the sha256 of each file under `canvas/project/ds/kinscape/` with `.design-system/project/`.

## Risks

- **Size:** see D1.
- **Touch:** a tap on a Pin comes as a MapLibre `click`. The phone e2e test proves it.
- **Board drift:** if the render changes a snapshot outside D4, stop.
