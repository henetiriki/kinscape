# Plan: M2.13.4: Pin popup on the map

- **Spec:** #862
- **Requirement IDs:** [PIN-02](../docs/requirements.md#pin-02), [NFR-05](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-05)
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

A shared `PinPopup` in `app/components/overlays/` builds the two draft boards: a floating form and a sheet form, each loaded, loading and failed. `MapView` reports a click on a Pin, a click on empty map and the pixel point of the open Pin on each map move. `pinsLayer` adds the selection ring as two circle layers that its filter limits to the open Pin. `MapShell` places the popup at the point on a desktop, and in the date sheet on a phone. `MapWithPins` holds the open Pin, and mounts the details query only when a Pin opens (#861). A `/screens` page builds the four boards with the components that the boards mount.

## Decisions

**D1 · The size of the Spec** · Decides: Tech lead · **Blocking**

The Spec estimates 650 lines. M2.13.2 (#1102) had the same estimate and changed about 1,780 lines. This plan counts about 1,400. The component and the design system take about 500, the map wiring and e2e about 650, and the screen page and entries about 250.

- **A.** Build it in one PR. Cost: more than twice the cap of about 600 lines.
- **B.** Split in two: (1) `PinPopup`, the `DateDock` slot, the token, the Components text and the design system steps; (2) the map wiring, the ring, the screen page, `screens.json` and the deletion of the draft boards. Cost: one new Spec, and #862 keeps part 2.
- **C.** Split in three: part 2 of B without the screen page and `screens.json`. Cost: two new Specs.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · The gap between a Pin and its popup** · Decides: Tech lead

The boards put the popup 30 px left of its Pin. `layout.popup-gap` is 14 px. The click popup of #868 takes the frame of `draft-pin-popup` (#860 plan D1) at a bare point, with no value for its gap.

- **A.** A new token `layout.pin-popup-gap`, 30 px. Cost: one token and its note.
- **B.** A literal 30 px in `PinPopup.module.css`, as the part's own dimension. Cost: #868 cannot share it.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Deviations

- `PinPopup` has a third form, `inline`, for the details in the date sheet (D5). The sheet form keeps its own handle.
- The ring is two circle layers from the `map.selected` tokens: a brand ring and a white `halo`. The tokens gain `halo` and a note. `radius` is the centre line, as the boards draw it.
- `MapView` takes a `select` list from each layer, and `Layer.filters` takes the open Pin id.
- The screen page has three routes, `map-pin-popup`, `/loading` and `/failed`. The address rule allows no `state` search parameter.
- Token and card counts change in `system.test.ts`, `cards.test.ts`, `design.test.ts`, `design-system.test.ts` and `hover.test.ts`.
- `docs/architecture.md` needs no change: the `overlays` folder is already in the React 18 and 19 row.
- `readFuzzyDate` and `formatFuzzyDate` take a `Pick` of the three fields they read. A Pin of the map has no more fields.
- `DateDock.module.css` gains `.rule`, the 1px line between the slider and the details.
- `PinPopupPage.tsx` draws the screen over the sea colour. `e2e/types.ts` types the box query that the e2e checks use.
- `react-18.test.tsx` and `bundle.test.ts` list `PinPopup` and `PlacedAt`. `screens.test.ts` has a `SCREEN_ONLY` row for `map-pin-popup`: the map has no loading or failed popup to show.
- The Pin popup text in `design/DESIGN.md` is five bullets, so each stays within the sentence limit. The card of `PinPopup` lists all five.
- `PlacedAt.tsx`, its CSS and its test are new. `Popup` and `PinPopup` share it. It stops the popup at `layout.furniture-top` and sets `z.popup` (D8, D11), so the Jurisdiction popup gets both too.
- `design/tokens.json` gains `z.popup` (D8).
- `useDismiss.ts` exports `isFreeEscape`, the Esc rule that `MapShell` shares with `useDismiss`.
- `lib/design/samples.mts` gains `PIN_POPUP` and `PIN_DETAILS`, which `states.mts` and `MapPinPopupScreen` share. `PinPopupState` is in `overlays/types.ts`, and the screen `types.ts` goes.
- `MapShell` closes the popup when the Pins row goes off, and expands the sheet when a Pin opens (D9, D10). `pins.ts` exports `PINS_LAYER_ID` for it.

The tech lead answered these in the review of #1104. The Deviations section has no word budget, so they sit here.
