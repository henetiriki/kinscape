# Plan: M2.13.5: Count marker and fan-out

- **Spec:** #863
- **Requirement IDs:** [PIN-06](../docs/requirements.md#pin-06), [NFR-01](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-01), [NFR-05](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-05)
- **Status:** draft

## Approach

A pure function groups the Pins by equal position (#863 D1). It splits each span into the days on which its Pin is alone on its Place. The `pins` source holds those parts, so a single Pin draws as now, and a Map date change only sets filters (NFR-01). Two shared components draw what the boards draw in HTML: `CountMarker` and `PinFan`. For a day, `pinsLayer` gives one `PlaceMarker` for each Place with two or more Pins. `MapView` places each with a MapLibre `Marker` and a portal (D2).

## Decisions

**D1 · The size of the Spec** · Decides: Tech lead · **Blocking**

The Spec estimates 450 lines. M2.13.4 estimated 650, and its plan counted 1,400. This plan counts about 1,300: 500 for the components and the design system, 550 for the map and e2e, 250 for the screen pages.

- **A.** One PR. Cost: about twice the cap of 600 lines.
- **B.** Two PRs: (1) the components, the design system steps, the screen pages and the board edits; (2) the map and e2e. Cost: one new Spec.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · How the map draws a count marker** · Decides: Tech lead

The boards draw the marker as an HTML button. A comment in `Map-pin-count.dc.html` says that a MapLibre circle layer draws the circle. The design check hides the map canvas.

- **A.** The button draws each look. Cost: one DOM element for each shared Place on the Map date.
- **B.** A circle layer draws the default look, and a clear button adds the target, the name and the other looks. Cost: two drawings that must line up.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D3 · How many Pins each ring holds** · Decides: Tech lead

The Spec gives ring 1 seven Pins, and each next ring 48 px more radius. Nothing gives the number for ring 2.

- **A.** Each ring keeps the gap of ring 1: 7 × radius ÷ 56, rounded down. Ring 2 holds 13.
- **B.** Each ring holds 7 more than the last: 7, 14, 21.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D4 · Where the Pins of a ring sit** · Decides: Tech lead

`map-pin-fan` and `map-pin-fan-phone` put 3 Pins at −150°, −30° and 90°: one below the Place. Ring 2 of `draft-pin-fan` has one below. Its full ring 1 has one above.

- **A.** Space each ring evenly, with one Pin below. Cost: ring 1 of `draft-pin-fan`, which goes, turns upside down.
- **B.** A full ring has one Pin above, and a ring that is not full has one below. Cost: two rules.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D5 · The order of the Pins in a fan** · Decides: Tech lead

No board or Spec gives an order. `PinFan` places the Pins in the order that it gets them.

- **A.** The map sorts them by span start, then id, clockwise from the Pin below. Cost: time order puts the 1890 Residence of `map-pin-fan` below, not at upper left. The screen page passes the board order.
- **B.** The order of the Pins route. Cost: it can change after a save.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D6 · The selection ring on a count marker** · Decides: Tech lead

No board draws the ring on a count marker. The `map.selected` ring (radius 16) fits the 22 px Pin, and covers the 2 px white border of the 32 px marker.

- **A.** `CountMarker` draws the `map.selected` ring, centred. Cost: a look that no board shows.
- **B.** Before the build, the tech lead draws a selected look on `Map-pin-count.dc.html`, which the Constraint allows. Cost: the build waits, and may need a token.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: B** (@henetiriki)

**D7 · The design system done checks** · Decides: Tech lead

D2: A makes both parts shared. `design/CANVAS.md` › Issue text then asks for two done checks, which #863 does not have.

- **A.** The build adds both checks to #863, as #862 has them. Cost: one reword.
- **B.** Leave #863. Cost: the Spec does not ask for the steps.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Alternatives considered

- MapLibre `cluster` groups by distance, before any filter. It cannot count by the Map date without a reload of the source.
- A new `setData` on each date change breaks "Load once, filter by expression".

9. The tech lead runs Design: Render snapshots. Run all checks. Compare the sha256 of each file under `canvas/project/ds/kinscape/` with `.design-system/project/`.

## Risks

- **Click:** MapLibre fires `click` for a click on a marker. `MapView` ignores it, so the popup does not close as it opens.
- **Hover gap:** 12 px lie between the marker and ring 1. An open `PinFan` holds a clear disc out to its outer ring.
- **Timing:** React renders portals before the effect that marks `map-date-change`. `MapView` sets the markers in that effect, after the mark, so `@perf` measures them.
- **Scale:** the seed shares few Places on one day, so `@perf` measures few markers.
- **Popup and fan:** a hover on the marker of the open Pin fans out over the popup. No board shows this.
- **Board drift:** if the render changes another snapshot, stop.
