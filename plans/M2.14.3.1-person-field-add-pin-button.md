- **Spec:** #866
- **Requirement IDs:** [PIN-01](../docs/requirements.md#pin-01), [PIN-02](../docs/requirements.md#pin-02), [NFR-05](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-05)
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

`PersonField` wraps `TextField` as an ARIA combobox. It filters with `findPeople` and lists `PickerRow`s in a 480 px list (D1, D2). The list sits in a `position: fixed` box, 4 px under the field box and on its left edge, because the panel body scrolls and would clip it. A pick sets the text to "Given Surname" and calls `onChange(person)`. Text after a pick calls `onChange(undefined)` and opens the list (Spec D1 A). `AddPinButton` is the primary `Button` named "Add a pin". The new `MapLayout` slot `action` places it 12 px left of the account control and hides it on a phone. Nothing mounts either part (Spec D2 A). #1123 wires both.

## Decisions

**D1 · Where the Person field lives** · Decides: Tech lead

The field needs `findPeople` (`lib/domain`) and `datesOf` (`features/people`). `app/components` may not import `lib/domain`. #1123 flags the same question for `FuzzyDateField`, which sits in `features/people` and in `CONTROLS`. #884 reuses the field.

- **A.** `features/people/components/PersonField.tsx`, exported from `features/people/index.ts`, as `FuzzyDateField` is. Cost: the forms of #1123 and #884 must sit in a layer that may import `features/people`.
- **B.** `app/components/forms/PersonField.tsx` with its own row type. The caller filters and passes the rows. Cost: #1123 and #884 each repeat the filter, and the filter checks test the caller, not the field.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · Where the focus goes on Down and Up** · Decides: Tech lead

`draft-pin-person-field` says "Down and Up move through the rows". It does not say where the keyboard focus goes. Each `PickerRow` is a button.

- **A.** The focus stays in the text box. `aria-activedescendant` names the active row, and that row shows the focus look. Down on a closed field opens the list. Cost: row buttons that never take the focus.
- **B.** Down moves the focus to a row button. Cost: a key on a row does not reach the text, so Spec D1 A needs code that returns the focus to the text box.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D3 · How the list closes without Esc or a pick** · Decides: Tech lead

The Spec closes the list on Esc and on a pick. The open list covers 480 × 176 px of the map.

- **A.** A press outside the field and the list closes it, and so does a focus move out of the field. The text and the pick stay. Cost: a rule that no board draws.
- **B.** Only Esc and a pick close it. Cost: the list stays over the map after the user goes to the next field.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D4 · How a card shows the open list** · Decides: Tech lead

A card state sets props only. The draft board shows the open list and the no-match notice.

- **A.** `PersonFieldList` is the list part, exported from `PersonField.tsx`. It has its own `CONTROLS` entry and card, as `MenuPanel` has for `Menu`. Cost: one more control.
- **B.** A `PersonField` prop opens the list with a given text. Cost: a prop that only the cards use.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D5 · The error look of the Person field** · Decides: Tech lead

`pin-form-errors` draws the field with the error "Choose a person." Step 7 swaps the field into that board. The Spec names no error look. #1123 shows the error and does not say who builds it.

- **A.** `PersonField` passes `error` and `aria-required` to `TextField`, with an error card state. Cost: about 10 lines.
- **B.** No error here. Step 7 keeps the hand-drawn field on `pin-form-errors`, and #1123 adds the prop. Cost: #1123 changes the control again.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Alternatives considered

- A list inside the panel body, with `position: absolute`: `overflow-y: auto` clips it at 320 px.
- A `max-height` of three `--size-row-l`: a row with a note is 58 px, so the third row is cut. The list measures its third row instead.

## Risks

- **Enter in a form:** Enter on an active row picks it and stops the event, so the form of #1123 does not submit.
- **Esc:** the field stops its own Esc, so the panel of #1123 stays open.
- **Fixed box:** an ancestor with a `transform` breaks `position: fixed`. No ancestor of the panel has one today. The box moves on scroll and resize. #1123's e2e proves it over the map.
- **Budget:** if the `design/DESIGN.md` budget fails, raise it in `scripts/word-count.mts` and record a Deviation.
- **Board drift:** if the render changes a snapshot that the plan does not list, stop.
