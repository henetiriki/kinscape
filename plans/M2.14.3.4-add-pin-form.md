# Plan: M2.14.3.4: Add a pin form at a new Place

- **Spec:** #1123
- **Requirement IDs:** [PIN-01](../docs/requirements.md#pin-01), [PIN-02](../docs/requirements.md#pin-02), [PIN-04](../docs/requirements.md#pin-04), [NFR-03](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-03), [NFR-05](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-05)
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

**The form.** `PinForm` (D1) is the panel of the `pin-form*` boards. It mounts `usePeople`, so the Persons load each time the panel opens. Until they arrive, it shows the `pin-edit-loading` look (D4). A failed load shows the `pin-edit-load-failed` look (D2). On Save it reads the text fields from `FormData`, because `FuzzyDateField` has no `onChange`.

**The save.** A new `parseCoordinates` in `lib/domain/places/` reads the coordinates. Then `checkPin` runs on the client, so each field shows its error and no request goes. A valid input goes to `onSave` with ids that `uuidV7()` made when the panel opened, so a retry saves no second Pin. A failed save shows the alert of `pin-form-save-failed`, and the mounted fields keep their values.

**The map.** A new hook `app/@map/useAddPin.tsx` holds the open state. On a desktop it gives `AddPinButton` while the panel is closed, and `PinForm` while it is open. On a phone it gives neither. `onSave` calls `createPinAction`, and a saved Pin calls `refetch` of `usePins`, also after Cancel or Esc closed the panel. `onSaved` closes the panel.

**The shell.** `MapShell` gets `action` and `panel` props, and `panel` takes the place of `LayersPanel`. `next.config.ts` gets a `private, no-store` rule for `/`.

**The Person field.** Its list renders through `createPortal` into `document.body`, so `--z-menu` counts above the dock. A scroll outside the list closes it (D5).

**The boards.** `MapFrame` gets `action` and `panel` props. `/screens/pin-form` mounts `useAddPin` in it. Each `pin-form*` entry signs in as a fixture Account: `r.family@example.com`, or `researcher@example.com` for `pin-form-no-people`. Its `state` opens and fills the panel. For saving and save failed, the state holds or aborts the action request with `page.route`.

**The sweep.** `sweepProblems` takes a fourth list, `IGNORE_UNTIL_867`, of the six `pin-form*` names. Each ignore area runs from the bottom of the address field and its error to the top of the footer.

The step 7 session sets the one Person of `Map-add-pin.dc.html` to Maria Magdalena du Toit. That also redraws `pin-edit` and `pin-edit-moved`, which #884 D4 A asks for. The build rewords #884.

## Decisions

**D1 · Where the Pin form lives** · Decides: Tech lead

The form needs `PersonField`, `FuzzyDateField` and `usePeople` of `features/people`. A feature may not import another feature (`docs/architecture.md` › Layers). #884 reuses the form.

- **A.** `features/people/components/PinForm.tsx`, with its stylesheet. `useAddPin` gives it `onSave` and `onSaved`. Cost: the people feature holds a Pin form.
- **B.** `app/@map/PinForm.tsx`, with a stylesheet, as `app/MapHost.module.css` has. Cost: UI with a stylesheet in the route layer, against the page rule.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · Error texts that no board draws** · Decides: Tech lead

`pin-form-errors` draws "Choose a person.", "Choose an event type.", the degrees, minutes and seconds text and "Use 500 characters or fewer." `pin-edit-load-failed` draws "Couldn't load this Pin". Four cases have no text.

- **A.** These texts:
  - an empty date: "Enter a date." (the Callout card in `lib/design/states.mts`);
  - empty coordinates: "Enter the coordinates.";
  - other text, or a point out of range: "Use decimal degrees, such as -33.93, 18.42.";
  - a failed load of the Persons: "Couldn't load your people", "The people of your Tree didn't load."
- **B.** The tech lead draws them on `pin-form-errors` and `pin-edit-load-failed` first. Cost: two more board edits, and #884's board changes.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D3 · The error of an empty date** · Decides: Tech lead

`FuzzyDateField` (in `CONTROLS`) gives no error for empty text, and has no `error` prop.

- **A.** It gets an `error` prop. The slot shows the field's own error first, then `error`. Its card gets the state. Cost: about 20 lines.
- **B.** With `aria-required`, its own check rejects empty text. Cost: the error shows when focus leaves the field, before Save, which no other field does.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D4 · The loading bars** · Decides: Tech lead

`pin-edit-loading` draws the 20px bars of the Pin popup. `PinPopup.tsx` holds them in a private `Bar`. A copied style is a stand-in ([missing-components rule](README.md#missing-components)).

- **A.** Move `Bar` to `app/components/feedback/LoadingBar.tsx`, with a card and a Components line. `PinPopup` and `PinForm` use it. Cost: about 50 lines, and one more part in `CONTROLS`.
- **B.** Export `Bar` from `PinPopup.tsx`. Cost: the form imports a part of the popup.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D5 · The Person list when its field scrolls away** · Decides: Tech lead

The #866 review found that the list then floats over the panel tabs or below the panel.

- **A.** A scroll outside the list closes it. Cost: the user opens it again.
- **B.** The list hides while the field is outside the panel body, and shows again. Cost: about 30 more lines that measure the body.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Deviations

- The fixture Trees are read-only. So `e2e/pin-form.spec.ts` holds only the save check. `e2e/fixture-pin-form.spec.ts` holds the other panel checks and the Person list checks.
- The seed holds 9,995 Pins, not 10,000 (the tech lead's answer in the review of #1129). A Pins list holds 10,000 Pins at most, so the save check now sees its new Pin in CI. `e2e/pins.spec.ts` accepts 9,995 or 9,996 in two counts, because the save check adds a Pin to the same Tree. The `@perf` test now says "about 10,000 Pins".
- `next.config.ts` also sets `private, no-store` on `/screens/pin-form`, because `noStoreBreaks` reads that page.
- The `PEOPLE` entry of `Map-add-pin.dc.html` also takes the dates and the maiden name of Maria Magdalena du Toit.
- The `ignore` areas have x 24 and width 320. Each starts below the address field and its error, and ends at y 791:
  - `pin-form`, `pin-form-empty` and `pin-form-saving`: y 636, height 155;
  - `pin-form-errors`: y 740, height 51;
  - `pin-form-save-failed`: y 696, height 95;
  - `pin-form-no-people`: y 746, height 45.
- `CoordinateError` is in `lib/domain/places/types.ts` and `PinMessages` is in `features/people/components/types.ts`: a lint rule asks for it.
- Esc closes the panel through a document listener. It acts only on an Esc that starts in the panel or on `body`, and that no control prevented. The Person list prevents its own Esc. An Esc in the date field of the dock or in the Pin popup does not close the panel.
- `FuzzyDateField` drops the `error` of the caller after a check that reads the date, and shows it again after an empty check.
