# Plan: M2.14.4: Precision, sources and notes on the form

- **Spec:** #867
- **Requirement IDs:** [PIN-02](../docs/requirements.md#pin-02)
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

**The fields.** `PinForm` gets three fields after the address, as `pin-form` draws them:

- the precision, a `SelectField` with `defaultValue='approximate'` and no empty choice;
- the sources and the notes, each a `TextAreaField` with `rows={2}`.

A new `PRECISION_OPTIONS` in `pin-messages.ts` holds the nine labels of the board. A test keeps its values equal to `PLACE_PRECISIONS`, as the test of `EVENT_OPTIONS` does.

**The save.** `readFields` reads the three values from `FormData`. The raw input gets `notes`, `sources` and `place.precision`. `checkPin` already gives `too-long` for the sources and the notes. For each, `pinMessages` gives "Use 10,000 characters or fewer.", the text of `pin-form-errors`. It writes the number from `MAX_SOURCES` and `MAX_NOTES` in `en-US` digit groups. `createPinAction` and the store need no change.

**The loading look.** `LOADING_FIELDS` gets three rows, as `pin-edit-loading` draws them (D2).

**The boards.** The six `pin-form*` entries lose their `ignore` areas. `fillPinForm` in `e2e/design-states.ts` also sets "Farm" and the sources and notes of `pin-form`. The `pin-form-errors` state fills both text areas with 10,001 characters of the board's text. #868 and #1057 add no name, so the list of the sweep goes (D1).

No design system input changes: `PinForm` is not in `CONTROLS`, and no board changes.

## Decisions

**D1 · The `mayIgnore` parameter of `sweepProblems`** · Decides: Tech lead

After this Spec removes the six names, `IGNORE_UNTIL_867` is empty. #1123 D5 A says this Spec removes the list. #868 keeps `pin-click-popup` off the list, and #1057 adds no name (#1123 D13 A). #868 does not wait for this Spec.

#868 D4 (open) asks whether `pin-click-popup` gets an `ignore` area. Its answered D1 A says yes. Under A here, #868 D4 must stay A (no area). If #868 D4 goes B, #868 adds the parameter and the list again, whichever Spec merges first.

- **A.** Remove `IGNORE_UNTIL_867`, the `mayIgnore` parameter and the test "accepts an ignore area on a listed entry only". `sweepProblems` then reports "has ignore" for each `ignore` area. Cost: a later Spec that needs an exception adds the parameter again.
- **B.** Remove `IGNORE_UNTIL_867`, and keep `mayIgnore` with its default `[]` and its test. Cost: a parameter that no caller fills.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · The loading look of the three fields** · Decides: Tech lead

#1123 D10 A gives the add panel the look of `pin-edit-loading`. That board draws eight rows: the five of `main`, then Precision (one bar of 120 px), Sources and Notes (bars of 220 and 160 px, 68 px high). #884 uses the same board for the edit panel. The Spec does not name the loading look.

`LoadingBar` is one 20 px bar with a width. It is not a row. The board draws a label over one or two stacked bars, with a 68 px minimum height. `PinForm` builds that row by hand (`.loading` and `.bars` in `PinForm.module.css`), and `PinPopup` builds its own bars. The [missing-components rule](README.md#missing-components) forbids a stand-in, and a style local to one form is a stand-in. So a row part may be missing.

- **A.** `LOADING_FIELDS` gets the three rows, with the widths of the board. The two-bar row is CSS in `PinForm.module.css`. Cost: about 15 lines, a one-off style, and no Acceptance check covers it.
- **B.** Build a `LoadingField` part in `feedback`: a label over one or more `LoadingBar` bars, with the 68 px minimum height. `LoadingBar` does not change. `PinForm` uses it for all eight rows. The part goes in `CONTROLS` with card states and a Components text in `design/DESIGN.md`, in its own commit with its test. The tech lead runs steps 3 to 7 of [Changing the design system](../design/CANVAS.md#changing-the-design-system) in a local session. Cost: about 100 more lines, under the 600-line cap, so no Spec of its own. `pin-edit-loading` already draws the row, so no draft board.
- **C.** The loading look keeps five rows. #884 adds the three rows. Cost: the add panel differs from `pin-edit-loading` until #884 merges.
- **D.** A Spec of its own builds `LoadingField` first, and this Spec waits for it. Cost: one more Spec. Choose it only if B passes the cap.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: B** (@henetiriki)

## Risks

- **Size.** About 250 lines, under the cap.
- **Scroll of a filled text area.** A `fill` puts the caret at the end, so the browser can show the end of the text. `pin-form-errors` draws the start of the text. The state sets `scrollTop` to 0 on both text areas and calls `blur()`.
- **Panel height.** The eight fields and the errors must fit above the footer at 900 px, as the boards draw. If the body scrolls, the design check fails. The `ignore` areas of `main` end at y 791, above the footer, so the boards show that the fields fit.
