# Plan: M2.5.3.2: Sign-in throttle

- **Spec:** #564
- **Requirement IDs:** [NFR-03](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-03)
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

Migration `0003` adds `sign_in_failure` (`email_key`, `failed_at`), indexed on `(email_key, failed_at)` and `failed_at`. A `ThrottleStore` port (add, list since, remove before) sits beside `SessionStore`, with memory and database adapters. `server/auth/throttle.mts` holds the rules over any store with an injected clock, as `sessions.mts` does: `emailKey` trims, lower-cases and (D1) hashes the email; `mayTry(store, now, email)` reads the last 30 minutes of failures and says whether the email is refused (D2); `countFailure(store, now, email)` adds a row and deletes rows older than 30 minutes, so the table stays small. `appAdapters()` gains `throttle`, memory under `KINSCAPE_MEMORY_ADAPTERS`.

## Decisions

**D1 · What a row keeps of the email** · Decides: Tech lead

Failures are kept for any email typed, including people who have no Account.

- **A.** The SHA-256 of the trimmed, lower-cased email. No address is stored; clearing an Account's rows needs `encode(sha256(convert_to('<email>', 'UTF8')), 'hex')`, which `docs/repo-settings.md` gives.
- **B.** The trimmed, lower-cased email. Clearing is a plain `where email = …`; `session` already keeps emails.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · When a refusal ends** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** 15 minutes after the failure that made 5 within 15 minutes. Rows live 30 minutes.
- **B.** While the last 15 minutes hold 5 failures. A refusal can end sooner than 15 minutes after it began; rows live 15 minutes.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Risks

- **Parallel guesses.** Many requests at once all pass `mayTry` before any failure is counted. M2.5.3.3 can count a failure before verifying and remove it on success; I'll flag it there.
- **Deliberate lockout.** As the Spec flags; `docs/repo-settings.md` says how to clear the rows.
- **Memory store across bundles.** Same risk as M2.5.2's sessions; M2.5.3.3 checks it.
- **Size.** About 350 changed lines; past 600, I stop (rule 7).
