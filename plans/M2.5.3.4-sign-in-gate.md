# Plan: M2.5.3.4: Sign-in gate

- **Spec:** #553
- **Requirement IDs:** [NFR-03](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-03)
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

`proxy.ts` redirects a request with no session cookie to `/sign-in`; its matcher skips `/sign-in`, `_next/`, `/data/` (D2) and paths with a file extension. `sessionOf` in `sessions.mts` is the full check: no token, a bad one or a store that throws gives `undefined`, so an outage fails closed. `currentSession()` reads the cookie and calls it; the map page redirects without a session, `/data/polities` answers 401, and the sign-in page drops its own copy. `openTree` takes the token instead of a `Viewer` and resumes the session itself (Spec D2); `treeAction` passes it on. `next.config` sets `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on `/:path*`. E2e shares one sign-in (D3); specs of `/sign-in` start signed out.

## Decisions

**D1 · Where the gate sends you** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** `/sign-in?returnTo=<path and query>`; M2.5.3.3's `safeReturnTo` checks it, so you land where you were going.
- **B.** Plain `/sign-in`; sign-in always opens the map.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · `/data/polities` without a cookie** · Decides: Tech lead

Behaviour sends "anyone" without a cookie to `/sign-in`, but the Acceptance check wants 401.

- **A.** The matcher skips `/data/`; the route answers 401 itself.
- **B.** `proxy.ts` answers 401 for `/data/` paths with no cookie, and the route checks again.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D3 · How e2e signs in** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** A Playwright `setup` project signs in once through the form as the synthetic Account and saves the state to `test-results/.auth/`; every project uses it.
- **B.** Each spec signs in through the form in `beforeEach`; about 180 extra sign-ins.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Order of work

1. `sessionOf` and `currentSession`, tests first.
2. `openTree` and `treeAction` on tokens.
3. Map page, `/data/polities`, sign-in page.
4. `proxy.ts`, `next.config`.
5. E2e setup project, signed-out specs, `gate.spec.ts`; docs.

## Risks

- MapLibre must send the cookie when it fetches `/data/polities`; same-origin fetch does, and the smoke test's "no failed request" proves it.
- Playwright's request context may not send a `Secure` cookie to `http://localhost`; if so, the e2e fetches from inside the page.
- Paths with a dot skip `proxy.ts`; no page has one, and pages check the session anyway.
