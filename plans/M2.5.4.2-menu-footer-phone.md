# Plan: M2.5.4.2: Menu: optional footer and phone form

- **Spec:** #570
- **Requirement IDs:** [NFR-03](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-03)
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

`footer` becomes optional on `MenuPanel` and `Menu`; the footer `span` renders only when it is set. `Menu` takes `form?: 'phone'` and sets `data-form` on its anchor and drop, as the variant pattern says. In `map-account-menu-phone` the pill's right edge is the gutter (16) and its bottom is 68. The Avatar's right edge is at 20 and its bottom at 64. The card's right edge is at 16 and its top at 76. So the phone card hangs from the pill, not from the Avatar. The desktop form does not change.

## Decisions

**D1 · Where the phone card hangs from** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** In the phone form the anchor is `position: static`, so the card hangs from the nearest positioned box. `SearchBar`'s `.pill` gets `position: relative`. The card is `right: 0` and `--layout-menu-offset` below the pill, which gives 16 and 76.
- **B.** The card stays on the Avatar and adds the pill's inset (`--space-4`) to `right` and `top`. `Menu` then repeats `SearchBar`'s padding.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · Phones narrower than 328** · Decides: Tech lead

The card is 296 wide. Below 328 it crosses the left gutter.

- **A.** The phone drop also takes `left: 0` and right-aligns the card, and the card takes `max-width: 100%`. The card is then never wider than the pill.
- **B.** No limit. Record the overlap as a risk.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D3 · Proof of the phone viewport check** · Decides: Tech lead

jsdom does no layout, and no route shows the phone form until #572.

- **A.** A unit test checks `data-form='phone'`. In #572, `design:check` on `map-account-menu-phone` proves the position.
- **B.** A phone sample on a kit board. This changes blocking snapshots, which the Spec's D2 excludes.
- **C.** A Playwright test on a new test-only route.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Order of work

1. Test a `Menu` and a `MenuPanel` with no footer: no footer line, the same groups. Watch it fail, then make `footer` optional.
2. Test the phone form's `data-form`, and run the arrow, Esc, Tab and outside-press tests with `it.each` over both forms. Watch the attribute test fail, then add the prop and CSS.
3. The pill rule.
4. Lint, typecheck, format, words, tests, `validate:boundaries`, e2e and `design:check`.

## Risks

Each one shows only once #572 puts the Menu in the pill. #572 fixes it.

- **Pill focus ring.** `.pill:focus-within` draws the ring while a menu item has focus. The snapshot has no ring.
- **Stacking.** Stage's `layers` slot comes after `search` with the same z-index, so the Layers button paints over the card.
- **No positioned box.** A phone-form `Menu` with no positioned ancestor hangs from the page. The prop's comment says this.
