# Plan: M2.5.4.4: Account menu on the map

- **Spec:** #572
- **Requirement IDs:** [NFR-03](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-03)
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

A new `AccountMenu` in `features/map` holds the two items and routes `onSelect`: `sources` opens the Sources dialog, `sign-out` calls `onSignOut` in `startTransition`, so a store failure reaches the error page (M2.5.4.1 D2 A). On a phone it draws `SearchBar form='pill'` with `Menu form='phone'` in its `account` slot; on desktop, the `Menu`. `MapShell` puts it in `account` (desktop) or `search` (phone) and takes `email`, `initial` and `onSignOut`. `app/page.tsx` passes the session's email, its initial (D1) and `signOutAction`. The route `/screens/map-account-menu` draws `Stage` with the same slots, the board's synthetic email and "L", and a `LayersButton` on a phone. A new design state opens the menu and hovers Sources and attributions. `Menu` does not change, so no design system step runs.

## Decisions

**D1 · The Avatar's letter** · Decides: PO

An Account has no name. The boards show "L" beside `researcher@example.com`.

- **A.** The first letter of the email, in upper case.
- **B.** A fixed letter for every Account.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D2 · Stacking of the menu slots** · Decides: Tech lead

The Spec's Flagged concerns name the `zoom` slot. No code fills `zoom` today.

- **A.** `Stage` gives `.search` and `.account` `z-index: var(--z-menu)` in both forms. One rule covers the phone Layers button, a later `zoom` and the overlay card.
- **B.** Only the phone `.search` gets it. A Spec that fills `zoom` fixes the desktop.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

## Risks

- **Shared e2e session.** The sign-out test starts with no storage state and signs in through the form (M2.5.4.1 plan).
- **Focus ring on the item.** A mouse click opens the menu in the state, so the scripted focus on the first item draws no `:focus-visible` ring. If it draws one, `design:check` fails; stop and ask.
