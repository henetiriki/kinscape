# Plan: M2.6: FuzzyDate

- **Spec:** #531
- **Requirement IDs:** [PIN-04](../docs/requirements.md#pin-04)
- **Status:** draft

## Approach

A new pure module, `lib/domain/dates/fuzzy-date.mts`, exports `parseFuzzyDate(text)`, which returns a `Result<FuzzyDate, FuzzyDateError>` and never throws, and `formatFuzzyDate(date)`. Parsing trims, collapses spaces and ignores case; formatting writes the GEDCOM form in capitals (`ABT 15 MAR 1850`). Date 1 and date 2 are stored as `1850`, `1850-03` or `1850-03-15`, which also gives the precision. Each date has a first and last day. ABT, EST and CAL move them 2 years out. BEF runs from 50 years before the first day to the day before it; AFT from the day after the last day to 50 years after it, as the acceptance checks give. BET and FROM run from date 1's first day to date 2's last. Moving by years keeps the month and day, and 29 Feb becomes 28 Feb in a common year. Days reuse `toDay` and `fromDay`. `Day` is a plain `number`, so births before 1652 get negative days (the Spec's flagged concern; tested).

## Decisions

**D1 · Which years are accepted** · Decides: Tech lead

GEDCOM allows years of 1–4 digits. `toDay` needs 4-digit ISO years, and AFT can add 50 years.

- **A.** Years 1000–2999; any other year is an error.
- **B.** Any GEDCOM year, with derived dates clamped to 0001–9999.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because every derived date stays a 4-digit ISO date.

**D2 · Ranges whose dates differ in precision** · Decides: Tech lead

In `BET 1848 AND MAR 1852`, the stored fields have one precision for two dates.

- **A.** Allow it. Each date keeps its own form, and `precision` is the coarser one (`year`).
- **B.** Return an error.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because GEDCOM allows it, register entries mix precisions, and the round trip still holds.

## Alternatives considered

- A Zod schema for the text: a hand-written matcher for this short grammar gives clearer error codes.
- `lib/domain/map/`: that folder is about the Map date. A Fuzzy date belongs to Persons and Pins.

## Risks

- **Inclusive vs half-open.** Boundary ranges are `[validFrom, validTo)`; a Fuzzy date's latest day is included. The type's comment and `architecture.md` say so, so M2.12's query uses `earliestDay <= day <= latestDay`.
- **Calendar.** Dates are proleptic Gregorian, as in `toDay`.
- **Size.** About 350 changed lines with tests.

## Out of scope

FROM or TO alone, dual years (`1750/51`), calendar escapes, B.C., `INT` and phrases: each is an error. Clipping to birth and death (M2.12). Entering dates (M2.9, M2.14). Fuzzy halos (EXP-02, M3).
