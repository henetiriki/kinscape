# Plan: M2.9.3: Kit: page header, action bar and Button link

- **Spec:** #797
- **Requirement IDs:** [PPL-01](../docs/requirements.md#ppl-01)
- **Status:** draft

## Approach

A `Button` with `href` draws an `<a>` with the same classes and `text-decoration: none`. The `NoticeCard` `.actions` rules also select `a`. `PageHeader` draws both Wordmark forms, and the phone media query shows one. `ActionBar` draws the fixed bar and an in-flow spacer of its height, which pads its page (D6). In the kit, a `contain: layout` box holds the fixed bar. Sizes come from `People-list` and `Person-new`, then from the draw.

## Decisions

**D1 · The component folders** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** `PageHeader` in `foundation`, `ActionBar` in `actions`.
- **B.** A new `page` folder, added to `DESIGN.md`, `docs/architecture.md` and `ROLE_FOLDERS` in `bundle.test.ts`.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because it adds no folder.

**D2 · Slots on a design system card** · Decides: Tech lead

A card `State` holds JSON props and text children only.

- **A.** `State` gets control slots and children, which `previewHtml` draws from `window.KinScape`.
- **B.** The `PageHeader` card has no Avatar. The `ActionBar` card has text children.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because B shows parts that no page draws.

**D3 · The back link states** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** `PageHeader.tsx` exports `BackLink`, with `state`, as a control with its own card.
- **B.** `PageHeader` takes `state`. The kit draws one header for each state.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because the board draws the link alone.

**D4 · The phone parts of `/design-kit/inputs`** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** Part 1 gets the `Button` links. Part 2 gets the other three sections. The draw follows this.
- **B.** Part 2 gets all four.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because the parts stay near one height.

## Order of work

1. Test first, then build: `Button`, `NoticeCard`, `BackLink`, `PageHeader`, `ActionBar`.
2. Tokens, `DESIGN.md`, `CONTROLS` and cards. The build agent then asks in the PR for step 3.
3. The tech lead runs the draw prompt on `SG-Inputs`, `SG-Inputs-phone`, `SG-Inputs-phone-2`, `SG-Surfaces` and `SG-Surfaces-phone`. A person runs "After the canvas". The tech lead checks the draw.
4. Kit sections and `screens.json`.
5. Design system step 2.
6. The tech lead, or a signed-in local Claude Code session that they start, runs steps 3 to 5.
7. Step 7 swaps `People-list`, `People-list-phone`, `Person-new`, `Person-form-phone`, `Person-not-found` and their importers: `People-empty`, `People-list-no-match`, `People-load-failed`, `Person-edit`, `Person-errors`, `Person-save-failed`, `Person-conflict`.
8. Steps 8 and 9, then the `AGENTS.md` checks.
