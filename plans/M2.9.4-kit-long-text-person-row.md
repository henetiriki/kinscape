# Plan: M2.9.4: Kit: long text field and Person row

- **Spec:** #798
- **Requirement IDs:** [PPL-01](../docs/requirements.md#ppl-01)
- **Status:** draft

## Approach

`TextAreaField` takes the `TextField` box looks with `composes`, and adds `resize: vertical`, `overflow-y: auto` and padding 10 12. `PersonRow` is one `<a>` with the `People-list` grid, gap, padding and `size.row-l` min-height. It takes strings and flags (`surname`, `givenNames`, `maidenName`, `matchedVariant`, `born`, `died`, `deceased`, `href`, `state`). As on `People-list`, one Small line joins the notes with " · ", and hidden "Born " and "Died " name the dates. Died shows `died`, then "Deceased" if `deceased`, then "—".

## Decisions

**D1 · The long text field component** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** A new `TextAreaField` in `forms`. It takes the box looks from `TextField.module.css`, as `SelectField` and `TokenField` do.
- **B.** `TextField` takes `multiline` and draws a `textarea`.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because the password, search, shortcut and date props of `TextField` do not apply to a `textarea`.

**D2 · The `PersonRow` folder** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** `actions`, beside `TextLink`: the row is a link.
- **B.** `foundation`, beside `BackLink`.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because `DESIGN.md` puts links that a person presses in `actions`.

**D3 · The pressed look of `PersonRow`** · Decides: Tech lead

The draw takes it from the other rows and links. On `main` these differ.

- **A.** `--segment` fill, as a `Menu` row.
- **B.** `--brand-tint` fill and `--brand-hover` "Edit", as `BackLink`.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because a `PersonRow` is a row of a list, as a `Menu` row is.

**D4 · The long text field on a phone** · Decides: Tech lead

The Spec gives Body 15/22. `TextField` uses 16/24 on a phone (`DESIGN.md` › Text field).

- **A.** 16/24 on a phone, as `TextField`. The Behaviour bullet and the draw prompt add "16/24 on a phone".
- **B.** 15/22 on every screen, as the Spec says.
- **Other:** your own.

Recommended: A, because iOS zooms in on a field with text under 16px.
