# Plan: M2.9.4: Kit: long text field and Person row

- **Spec:** #798
- **Requirement IDs:** [PPL-01](../docs/requirements.md#ppl-01)
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

`TextAreaField` takes the `TextField` box looks with `composes`. `PersonRow` is one `<a>` with the `People-list` grid, gap, padding and `size.row-l` min-height. As on `People-list`, one Small line joins the notes with " · ", and hidden "Born " and "Died " name the dates.

## Decisions

**D1 · The long text field component** · Decides: Tech lead

- **A.** A new `TextAreaField` in `forms`. It takes the box looks from `TextField.module.css`, as `SelectField` and `TokenField` do.
- **B.** `TextField` takes `multiline` and draws a `textarea`.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: A** (@henetiriki)

**D3 · The pressed look of `PersonRow`** · Decides: Tech lead

The draw takes it from the other rows and links. On `main` these differ.

- **A.** `--segment` fill, as a `Menu` row.
- **B.** `--brand-tint` fill and `--brand-hover` "Edit", as `BackLink`.
- **Other:** your own.

**Answer: Other:** --segment fill on the row, as a Menu row, and the "Edit" label takes --brand-pressed, as TextLink. (@henetiriki)
