# Plan: M2.9.6: People list

- **Spec:** #801
- **Requirement IDs:** [PPL-01](../docs/requirements.md#ppl-01), [NFR-09](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-09), [NFR-10](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-10)
- **Status:** draft

## Approach

`app/people/page.tsx` calls `requireSession`, then reads Persons through `openTree`. It gives `PeopleList` one `PersonSummary` per Person, so notes stay on the server. A thrown read gives the load-failed state. `PeopleList` (`features/people`) sorts with `Intl.Collator`, filters, counts and draws the rows or a notice. `next.config.ts` sets the `/people` header. `.row` gets `position: relative`, so the hidden labels stay inside the row.
