# Plan: M2.9.7: People on a phone and in the account menu

- **Spec:** #803
- **Requirement IDs:** [PPL-01](../docs/requirements.md#ppl-01), [NFR-05](../docs/requirements.md#nfr-05)
- **Status:** approved

## Approach

`PersonRow` gets `form='phone'`: a `div` with the name, the note and one Mono line. `PeopleList` calls `usePhone` and draws `People-list-phone`. A new client `EditOnComputer` wraps each Person form route and shows the notice on a phone. The map's `AccountMenu` adds the People group and pushes `/people`. About 560 changed lines.
