## Approach

`PersonForm` (`features/people`) is a client form. The pages pass it a server action and, to edit, the Person. Save runs `checkPerson` before a request. `/people/[id]` reads the Person with `tree.persons.get` through `treeAction`. A bad id or no Person gives the not-found state. A new form makes a UUIDv7 id once, so "Try again" repeats the same create.

## Deviations

- `PersonNotFound` is a new component in `features/people`, because the not-found page needs the client `AccountMenu`.
- `person-errors` shares the route `/screens/person-new`; its state fills the dates and presses Save.
- Dev pages call stand-in actions in `app/screens/person-actions.ts` for the save-failed and conflict states.
- `app/components/__tests__/hover.test.ts` and `lib/design/__tests__/design.test.ts` have new file counts.
- `fuzzy-date-readings` is sized 1440x1255, as its snapshot is. It differs by 3.09%, over the 0.5% limit. The table rows sit about 2px off the board.
- The kit variant comes before the draw, because the board copies the variant caption.
- `SG-Inputs` is 7483px. `canvas.json` moves the style guide boards below it down by 198px.
- `screens.json` keeps 7285 for `style-6-components-inputs` until Design: Render snapshots sets the JPG height.
- The swaps of `TokenField` on `SG-Inputs` and `CheckboxField` on `Person-new` change pixels. Both boards stay hand-drawn.
